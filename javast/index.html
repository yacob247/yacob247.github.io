What's inside

Everything in one file

Hand-written GLSL ES 3.0, custom matrix math, custom geometry — no imports, no CDN, no build step.

Gerstner wave stack

Six stacked waves with deep-water dispersion and irrational wavelength ratios so the surface never tiles.

PBR water shading

GGX specular, Schlick Fresnel, Beer-Lambert absorption, subsurface scattering on crests and sky-matched reflections.

Underwater world

Dive below the surface and the whole scene switches: exponential fog, Snell's window, dimmed sky, blue-shifted lighting.

Procedural fish

Parametric body with proper radial profile, five fin sheets, per-eye sphere geometry, and vertex-shader swimming motion.

Seabed with caustics

Analytic dunes, four-octave value noise sand color, and animated caustic light ribbons under the water.

Free camera + swim

Pointer-lock mouse look, WASD movement, Space/Shift vertical. Fly above water or dive below with the same controls.
