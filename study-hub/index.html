What is grammar?

Grammar is the system of rules a language uses to combine words into meaningful units — words into phrases, phrases into clauses, clauses into sentences, and sentences into texts. In the NESA syllabus, grammar knowledge sits under Objective C: think in ways that are imaginative, interpretive and critical and outcome EN12-5: "thinks imaginatively, creatively, interpretively and critically about information and ideas when responding to and composing texts." Grammar is not an arbitrary set of prohibitions; it is a descriptive description of how meaning is made. Understanding it gives you conscious control over your writing so you can choose structures deliberately for effect.
