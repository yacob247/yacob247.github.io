The Complete Encyclopedia of World Civilizations

From the first cities of Sumer on the banks of the Euphrates to the empire of the Incas in the high Andes — the full story of human civilisation across every continent. Each civilization is covered in depth with original, referenced prose, every key term explained, every timeline date expanded into a flipping study card, and every significant individual given their own page. This is one unified history website: browse Ancient Greece, Ancient Rome, or the Civilizations below.

How to use this library. Each civilization is its own complete article. Hover or tap any highlighted term to see its definition. Click any date in a timeline to flip a study card that explains the event in 200–500 words. Click a person's name to open that individual's own page. Every fact is supported by in-text citations pointing to the World History Encyclopedia, Encyclopaedia Britannica, museum and university resources — with direct, working URLs.
