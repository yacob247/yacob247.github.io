I. Overview

Son of Pachacuti, Topa marched 5,000 km: crushed the Chimú adobe empire and moved its smiths to Cuzco; took Ecuador after bloody Cañari wars; pushed to Chileʼs Maule where Mapuche halted him. Empire became worldʼs largest. Died 1493, perhaps poisoned by a wifeʼs faction (WHE, Inca).

1470 — Adobe Capital Taken

Damned Chan Chanʼs canals, starved the largest adobe city on earth. Chimú king Minchancaman led to Cuzco in honourable captivity; goldsmiths resettled. North coast joined Tawantinsuyu.

Legend — Islands of Gold

Chronicles say Topa sailed with 20,000 men on balsa rafts to “Nina Chumpi” and “Hahua Chumpi,” returning with gold, a brass throne and dark captives. Some link to Galápagos or Polynesia — unproven but persistent.
