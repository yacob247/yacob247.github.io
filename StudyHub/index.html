    Why These Packs?

    Each pack follows the same trusted structure: a full text-type guide plus a ten-lesson program. Every lesson includes a stimulus, an exam-style question, starter sentences, a practice worksheet, a Band 6 sample answer, and NESA-aligned marking criteria. The guides connect everything to the current NSW English syllabus and Module C: The Craft of Writing.
