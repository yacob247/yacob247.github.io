    What Is Discursive Writing?

    Discursive writing is a mode of composition in which an author explores an idea or question from multiple perspectives without committing to a single forceful conclusion. It is valued in Module C for its sincerity, balance and personal voice. These resources will take you from the basics to a Band 6 level.
