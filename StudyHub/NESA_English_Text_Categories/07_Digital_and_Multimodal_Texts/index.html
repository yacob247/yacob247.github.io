What Are Digital & Multimodal Texts?

Multimodal texts combine two or more modes — written, visual, audio, gesture and spatial — while digital texts add interactivity, hyperlinks and networks. In Stage 6 English, students analyse websites, digital storytelling, blogs and interactive media, reading the ‘grammar’ of the screen and their own role as a user.
