10-Lesson Digital & Multimodal Program

Ten lessons from the five modes to making your own multimodal text — each with an analysis task and exam-style question.

Lesson 1

Modes and Meaning

Teaching focus: The written, visual, audio, gesture and spatial modes and how they combine.

Exam-style question: Identify the modes in a screen and explain how they work together.

Starter sentences: “The synergy of image and text…” · “The audio mode contributes…”

Activity: Capture a screen and label each mode you find.

Lesson 2

The Grammar of the Screen

Teaching focus: Layout, hierarchy, colour, font and composition as meanings.

Exam-style question: Analyse how the page’s layout positions the reader.

Starter sentences: “The prominence given to…” · “White space directs attention to…”

Activity: Compare the graphical design of two contrasting websites.

Lesson 3

Reading a Website

Teaching focus: Navigation, hierarchy, branding and how a site builds trust and identity.

Exam-style question: Analyse how a website constructs its identity and its audience.

Starter sentences: “The navigation implies…” · “From its branding, the site…”

Activity: Deconstruct one website’s home page for identity, audience and rhetoric.

Lesson 4

Interactivity and the User

Teaching focus: How choice, scrolling and feedback make the user a co-constructor of the text.

Exam-style question: Discuss the role of the user in making meaning from the text.

Starter sentences: “By choosing to… the user…” · “The interface invites the visitor to…”

Activity: Test an interactive feature and record the choices it offers you.

Lesson 5

Digital Storytelling

Teaching focus: How narratives are built from image, audio and text over a digital medium.

Exam-style question: Analyse how the digital format shapes a story’s telling.

Starter sentences: “Scrolling enacts … of the plot…” · “The soundtrack establishes…”

Activity: Watch a short digital story and map how each mode advances it.

Lesson 6

Blogs, Vlogs and Personal Voice

Teaching focus: The construction of authenticity, seriality and community in blog and vlog texts.

Exam-style question: Analyse how a vlog constructs its creator’s personal brand.

Starter sentences: “The claimed intimacy of direct address…”

Activity: Study one vlogger’s content for techniques that build parasocial trust.

Lesson 7

Social Media and the Feed

Teaching focus: Brevity, algorithm, virality and the engineered loops of social platforms.

Exam-style question: Evaluate how a platform shapes the form of its posts.

Starter sentences: “Designed for the scroll…” · “The algorithm rewards…”

Activity: Analyse the design constraints of one platform post type.

Lesson 8

Hyperlinks and Networks

Teaching focus: How links create meaning, structure argument and draw on the whole web.

Exam-style question: Analyse how hyperlinking shapes the reading experience.

Starter sentences: “The outbound link to… lends…” · “Following this link, the reader…”

Activity: Map the links in an article and note what each one implies.

Lesson 9

Persuasion and Design

Teaching focus: How digital texts move users to act — through calls, urgency and design.

Exam-style question: Analyse the persuasive strategies of a landing page.

Starter sentences: “The call-to-action is positioned…” · “Urgency is created by…”

Activity: A/B review two landing pages for their persuasive machinery.

Lesson 10

Creating and Evaluating a Multimodal Text

Teaching focus: Planning, producing, and evaluating an original digital text against criteria.

Exam-style question: Create a digital text for a purpose, then write a reflective evaluation of your multimodal choices.

Band 6 checklist: clear purpose, deliberate mode choices, coherent design, understanding of audience and context.

Activity: Design a short web page or digital story and evaluate it in writing.

Marking guidance: Use NESA Stage 6 marking guidelines and align assessments to outcomes for representing and composing in digital media.
