Syntax in One Minute

Syntax is the arrangement of words into sentences. Composers control it the way musicians control tempo: short sentences punch; long sentences accumulate; subordination ranks importance; fronting sets the frame. When you can name what a sentence's shape does, you have analysis that most students never write.

I have taken more out of alcohol than alcohol has taken out of me.— attributed to Winston Churchill — a balanced sentence whose mirrored clauses are its entire wit.
