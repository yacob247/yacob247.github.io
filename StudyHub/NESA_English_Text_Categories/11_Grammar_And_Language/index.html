How These Packs Fit Syllabus

Every pack is mapped to the NESA English K–10 Syllabus (2022) outcomes and the Stage 6 English syllabuses. Grammar knowledge supports outcome EN4-9B / EN5-9B-style understanding of language; technique analysis underpins responding and composing outcomes; and the course guides align directly with the current Stage 6 prescriptions published by NESA. Always confirm your cohort's prescriptions on the official site before assessment periods.
