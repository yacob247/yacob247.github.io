What Are Imaginative Texts?

Imaginative texts create made-up worlds and events to entertain and explore experience. Across K–10 they include novels, picture books, poetry, plays, fairy and traditional stories, and narrative films. They develop creativity, empathy and a love of story, and their purposes and features can be analysed and produced by students at every level.
