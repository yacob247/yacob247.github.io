    Start Here

    If you are unsure what “Band 6” legally means, read How Band 6 Actually Works below first. It explains the 50:50 exam/school-based split, why Band 6 is not the same as a raw 100 in the paper, and what NESA’s published descriptors are actually written to describe.

    “Your mark in each course is a 50:50 combination of your HSC exam mark and your school-based assessment mark.”

    “Each student’s exam is marked, and then their achievement is reported using a set standard of performance for each course. Judging is a process that matches a student’s raw exam marks to the standards. The standards describe what a student knows and can do. Student achievement is related to the standards, not other students.”

    NESA, Understanding your HSC results — NSW Government [source]
