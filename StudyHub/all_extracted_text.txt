The Complete Guide to Discursive Writing What Is Discursive Writing? Discursive writing is a mode of expression in which the author explores a topic from multiple angles without committing wholesale to a single conclusion. The word derives from the Latin discursus — literally "a running to and fro" — which neatly captures the way a discursive essay moves back and forth between competing ideas, weighing evidence, considering perspectives, and only lightly settling on a nuanced viewpoint.
