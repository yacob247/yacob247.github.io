Every Individual in Greek History

A complete directory of the men and women who shaped Greek civilisation — kings, tyrants, lawgivers, generals, philosophers, scientists, dramatists, poets, historians and orators — with dates, role and significance.

Scope & method. This directory includes the individuals whose lives and works are documented or traditionally recorded as significant in Greek history. Dates are those generally given in standard reference works; "c." indicates approximate dates. Because the full biographical detail of every one of these figures is vast, each entry states dates, role, and significance, and where possible links to a detailed external article.

A. Mythical Founders, Heroes & Epic Figures

NameDateRole & Significance
Achilleslegendary (Trojan War era, c. 1250 BC)Greatest Greek hero of the Trojan War; hero of Homer's Iliad; archetype of the warrior and of rage.
Agamemnonlegendary (c. 1250 BC)King of Mycenae, commander of the Greek expedition against Troy; central figure of Greek epic tragedy.
AeneaslegendaryTrojan hero, son of Aphrodite, who (in Virgil) fled Troy to become ancestor of the Romans.
AjaxlegendaryGiant Greek hero at Troy, famed for strength; subject of Sophocles' tragedy.

Helen of Troylegendary"The face that launched a thousand ships"; Spartan queen whose abduction caused the Trojan War.
HectorlegendaryTrojan prince and greatest Trojan warrior, son of Priam; hero of the Iliad.
Heracles (Hercules)legendaryThe greatest Hellenic hero, son of Zeus; the Twelve Labours; ancestor of the royal houses including the Argeads of Macedon.
JasonlegendaryLeader of the Argonauts in quest of the Golden Fleece; husband of Medea.
MinoslegendaryLegendary king of Crete, lawgiver, son of Zeus and Europa; associated with the Labyrinth and Minotaur; gave the name "Minoan" to the Bronze Age civilisation.

OrpheuslegendaryLegendary musician and poet whose song moved the gods and stones; founder of Orphic mysteries.
PriamlegendaryLast king of Troy; father of Hector and Paris; killed at the fall of Troy.
SisyphuslegendaryMythical founder of Corinth, condemned eternally to roll a boulder uphill in Hades.
TheseuslegendaryLegendary king and hero of Athens; slew the Minotaur; "synoecist" who united Attica; ancestor of Athenian demos.
