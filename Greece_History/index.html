The Complete Encyclopedia of Ancient Greece

From the first palaces of Minoan Crete to the last Roman emperors in Constantinople — the full three-thousand-year history of the Greeks, every significant individual, every decisive event, every idea that shaped the world, each entry with inline citations and direct links to trusted sources.

How to use this library. This is an encyclopaedia, not a study guide. Every page presents a full chronological narrative of Greek history or a complete reference directory of the people and events that made it. Dates before c. 800 BC are approximate and debated (the traditional chronology is used). Each fact is supported by in-text citations pointing to authoritative open sources — primarily the World History Encyclopedia, the Loeb and Perseus digital libraries, and museum & university resources — with direct, working URLs.

III. Sources & Method

This encyclopaedia is compiled from established scholarly and reference works. The primary online source is the World History Encyclopedia (worldhistory.org), which is reviewed for educational use by Oxford University and others, alongside the Perseus Digital Library, the Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, the Livius encyclopedia, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, and the British Museum. Direct links to every cited page are given throughout the text.
