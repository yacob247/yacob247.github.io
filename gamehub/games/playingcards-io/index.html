About PlayingCards.io

PlayingCards.io is a virtual table for playing cards and board games with friends. It supports many built-in games and also lets you create custom tables with your own cards and rules. It is the most flexible way to recreate physical game nights online.

How to play

Pick a built-in game or create a custom room. Share the room link with friends so they can join. Interact with cards and pieces by dragging and clicking. Most games have clear rules built in, and custom rooms let you define everything yourself. It is a sandbox for games, so the rules are whatever you and your group agree on.

Game modes

Multiplayer virtual tables with built-in games and custom room creation.

Tips and tricks

  1. Use the built-in templates as a starting point for custom games.
  2. Share the room link before starting so everyone can join.
  3. Agree on rules verbally for custom games.
  4. Use the chat to communicate during play.

Features

  • Virtual table for cards and board games.
  • Built-in games plus fully custom rooms.
  • Play with friends via shared links.
  • Free to play in the browser.
