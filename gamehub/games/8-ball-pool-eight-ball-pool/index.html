About 8-Ball Pool

8-Ball Pool is a classic cue sport where you clear your assigned group of balls and sink the 8-ball to win. Play against the computer in this polished browser version and refine your aiming, spin, and positional play.

How to play

Take turns with your opponent, aiming and shooting the cue ball to pocket your group of balls and eventually the 8-ball. Use spin, angles, and power to control the cue ball. Sink all your group's balls first, then pocket the 8-ball to win. Fouls give your opponent a free ball.

Game modes

Single-player matches against AI with adjustable difficulty.

Tips and tricks

  1. Learn basic angle geometry for consistent shots.
  2. Use spin to control the cue ball's position.
  3. Play safe when you cannot make a clear shot.
  4. Plan two shots ahead.
