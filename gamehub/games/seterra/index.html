About Seterra

Seterra is a geography quiz platform with interactive map games covering countries, capitals, flags, and physical features. Whether you are a student, a traveler, or a trivia fan, it is a fun and effective way to learn world geography.

How to play

Choose a quiz category — countries, capitals, flags, rivers, and more. Interactive maps and multiple-choice questions test your knowledge. You earn points for correct answers and can track your progress. The quizzes are timed and scored, so you can compete with yourself or others.

Game modes

Single-player geography quizzes across many categories and difficulty levels.

Tips and tricks

  1. Start with your own continent before moving to others.
  2. Repeat quizzes to reinforce memory.
  3. Use the map hints to learn locations.
  4. Set a goal to improve your score each session.

Features
