About Generals.io

Generals.io is a tight, fast-moving strategy game about territory, supply lines, and outmaneuvering your opponent. Every tile matters, every move reveals information, and the simplest-looking decisions can decide the whole match. It is easy to learn in a minute and deep enough to keep you thinking for hours.

How to play

You begin with a general tile and some land. Each turn, your tiles generate armies based on their value. Move armies between adjacent tiles to expand into neutral territory or attack your opponent. Capture enemy land, cut off their supply routes, and eventually take their general to win. Fog of war hides much of the map, so scouting and guessing your opponent's plans is a core part of the game. Moves happen simultaneously each turn, so prediction matters.

Game modes

Multiplayer matches including 1v1 and free-for-all, with ranked and casual play options.

Tips and tricks

  1. Do not overextend — thin armies get cut off and destroyed easily.
  2. Control high-value tiles early; they generate the most armies.
  3. Watch for gaps in your opponent's territory that reveal their general.
  4. Keep some armies in reserve to defend against sudden attacks.
  5. In free-for-all, let others fight first and expand when they are weakened.

Features

  • Fog of war that makes scouting and prediction essential.
