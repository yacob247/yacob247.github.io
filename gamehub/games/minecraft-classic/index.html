About Minecraft Classic

Minecraft Classic is the official free, browser-based version of the game that started a worldwide phenomenon. With no survival pressure and no monsters, it is pure creative sandbox building — just you, an infinite block world, and the freedom to construct anything you can imagine. It is also a great way to build and explore with up to ten friends at once.

How to play

You spawn into a block world with an unlimited supply of blocks. Choose a block type from the hotbar, then place and break blocks to build whatever you want. There is no hunger, no health, and no danger — the focus is entirely on creativity. Explore the generated terrain, carve out caves, build structures, or work together with other players in the same world. Everything you create exists only for that session unless it is shared.

Game modes

Creative sandbox play with support for multiplayer worlds of up to 10 players.

Tips and tricks

  1. Plan large builds roughly before you start so you do not waste time.
  2. Use contrasting blocks to outline shapes before filling them in.
  3. Build from the ground up and check your proportions from a distance.
  4. In multiplayer, divide big builds between players to finish faster.
  5. Experiment with block combinations — texture mixing makes builds look better.

Features

  • Multiplayer worlds for up to 10 players.
  • No monsters, hunger, or survival pressure.
