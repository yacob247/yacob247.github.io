About Deeeep.io

Deeeep.io is an ocean survival and evolution game where you start as a tiny sea creature and climb the food chain. Swim, eat, and evolve into larger and more powerful animals while avoiding predators and fighting for survival in a vast underwater world. With many creatures and biomes, it is a deep and rewarding experience.

How to play

Choose your starting creature and dive into the ocean. Eat food and smaller creatures to gain experience and evolve into bigger forms. Each evolution unlocks new abilities and stats. Avoid larger predators and use the environment — reefs, deep water, and caves — to survive. Hunt strategically and explore different biomes for rare food. The goal is to reach the top of the food chain.

Game modes

Multiplayer ocean survival with evolution and combat.

Tips and tricks

  1. Stay in shallow water early to avoid deep-sea predators.
  2. Evolve as quickly as possible to escape the bottom of the food chain.
  3. Use terrain to escape larger creatures.
  4. Learn which creatures are safe to approach and which are deadly.
  5. Explore deeper waters only when you are strong enough.

Features

  • Ocean survival with evolution progression.

  • Multiplayer competition with a food chain.
  • Unique abilities for different creatures.
