About Gats.io

Gats.io is a snappy top-down shooter with a class system that gives every match a fresh tactical feel. Choose a loadout, pick your fights, and battle across a compact map where positioning and timing matter more than raw reflexes. With its minimalist visuals and deep class variety, it is a game that rewards smart play and quick thinking.

How to play

Choose a class with its own weapon and stat profile, then spawn into the map and fight other players. Move around the arena using cover and terrain to control engagements. Each class has strengths and weaknesses — a sniper wins at range while a shotgun dominates close up. Collect powerups and use your mobility to outmaneuver opponents. Be the last player standing or help your team reach the objective depending on the mode.

Game modes

Free-for-all and team-based top-down combat with multiple classes.

Tips and tricks

  1. Play to your class's range — do not fight close with a sniper or long with a shotgun.
  2. Use walls and cover to break line of sight before re-engaging.
  3. Switch classes between lives to counter what the enemy team is running.
  4. Keep moving — top-down games punish players who stand still.
  5. Learn when to disengage; retreating to reset a fight is often the best play.
