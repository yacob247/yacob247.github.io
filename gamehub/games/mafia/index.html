About Mafia

Mafia is a cinematic crime epic set in the 1930s, where fast cars, sharper suits, and dangerous loyalties define every day. You are pulled into a world of bootlegging, extortion, and betrayal, and must survive long enough to earn your place among men who do not forgive mistakes. With atmospheric city streets, weighty driving physics, and a story-driven campaign, it is a classic mob drama you can play.

How to play

Follow the story mission by mission, completing jobs that range from chauffeuring and deliveries to robberies and shootouts. Drive period-accurate cars through the city — and be aware that they handle heavily and break down if abused. In combat, use cover, aim carefully, and manage your ammunition. Between missions you can explore the city, but the campaign is the heart of the experience. Complete each chapter to see the story unfold.

Game modes

Single-player story campaign set in an open city with mission-based progression.

Tips and tricks

  1. Drive carefully — police notice reckless driving and cars take damage fast.
  2. Use cover in every firefight; standing in the open is a death sentence.
  3. Conserve ammunition by aiming deliberately rather than spraying.
  4. Learn the city layout so you can escape police pursuits quickly.
  5. Save between missions; failing mid-mission can cost you a long drive.

Features

  • A story-driven campaign set in a richly detailed 1930s city.
  • Period-accurate vehicles with weighty driving physics.
  • Cover-based gunfights and tactical combat.
  • Runs in-browser through the BottleShip player.
  • An atmospheric crime drama with memorable characters.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mafia?

Mafia is a classic story-driven crime game set in the 1930s, following a man drawn into the underworld of organized crime.

Do I need to install it?

No. It runs directly in your browser via the BottleShip player.

Is the driving realistic?

The cars have deliberately heavy, period-appropriate handling, so you need to brake early and drive with care.
