About Wings.io

Wings.io puts you in the cockpit of a fast, agile fighter plane for multiplayer dogfights in the sky. Loop, dive, and outturn your opponents in a game that is all about aerial positioning and quick reflexes. With arcade flight controls and fast-paced combat, it turns the classic dogfight into something you can enjoy straight from your browser.

How to play

Take to the skies and engage other players in aerial combat. Control your plane's pitch, roll, and throttle to outmaneuver opponents. Get behind enemy planes and fire your guns to bring them down. Use the environment and altitude to your advantage, and avoid getting caught in a turn fight you cannot win. Take down as many opponents as possible while staying alive yourself.

Game modes

Multiplayer free-for-all aerial dogfighting.

Tips and tricks

  1. Always try to get behind your target — head-on attacks are a coin flip.
  2. Use altitude for energy; diving converts height into speed and options.
  3. Break off a losing turn fight and reset rather than committing to it.
  4. Keep an eye on your surroundings; third parties often show up mid-duel.
  5. Learn to lead your shots — planes move quickly and bullets take time to travel.
