About Mope.io

Mope.io is a survival and evolution game where you start as a tiny animal and climb the food chain. Eat, grow, and evolve into bigger and more powerful creatures while avoiding predators and fighting for territory. With a huge variety of animals and biomes, every session is a new adventure in the wild.

How to play

Choose your starting animal and spawn into the world. Eat food and smaller animals to gain experience and evolve into larger creatures. Each evolution tier unlocks new abilities and options. Avoid larger predators — they will eat you. Use terrain and water to your advantage, and hunt strategically. The goal is to reach the top of the food chain and dominate your biome.

Game modes

Multiplayer survival and evolution with many animal tiers and biomes.

Tips and tricks

  1. Stay near food sources early to evolve quickly.
  2. Avoid players at higher tiers — they can one-shot you.
  3. Use water and mud to escape predators.
  4. Learn which animals can eat which; the food chain is strict.
  5. Do not get greedy near the top — one mistake can send you back to the start.

Features

  • Abilities and special moves for each animal.
