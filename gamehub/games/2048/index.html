About 2048

2048 is the addictive sliding tile puzzle that took the world by storm. Combine matching numbers to reach the 2048 tile, and beyond. Simple to learn, hard to master, and endlessly replayable, it is one of the most popular puzzle games ever made.

How to play

Slide tiles on a 4x4 grid using the arrow keys. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value. After each move, a new tile appears. Keep merging to reach 2048 — and then keep going for a higher score. The game ends when the board fills and no moves are possible.

Game modes

Single-player endless puzzle with score chasing.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep your highest tile in a corner.
  2. Avoid moving the highest tile unnecessarily.
  3. Build rows and columns in a snake pattern.
  4. Do not chase small merges that break your structure.

Features

  • Classic sliding tile puzzle.
