About Reversi

Reversi (also known as Othello) is a classic strategy board game where you flip your opponent's discs by outflanking them. Simple to learn but deeply strategic, it is a great game for players who enjoy positional thinking.

How to play

Place discs on the board to outflank your opponent's discs, flipping them to your color. Each move must flip at least one opponent disc. The player with the most discs of their color at the end wins. Control corners and edges for maximum advantage.

Game modes

Single-player against AI and multiplayer.

Tips and tricks

  1. Corners are the most valuable positions.
  2. Avoid giving your opponent corner access.
  3. Control the edges.
  4. Think about mobility, not just disc count.
