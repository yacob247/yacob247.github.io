About Chess

Chess is the ultimate game of strategy, tactics, and foresight. Play against a calculating AI opponent in your browser and test your opening knowledge, middlegame plans, and endgame skill. Whether you are learning the basics or sharpening your game, it is a timeless challenge.

How to play

Each player starts with 16 pieces. Move your pieces according to their rules and try to checkmate the opponent's king. Pieces have different movement patterns: pawns, knights, bishops, rooks, queens, and kings. Castling, en passant, and promotion add depth. Checkmate your opponent to win.

Game modes

Single-player against AI with adjustable difficulty.

Tips and tricks

  1. Control the center early.
  2. Develop your pieces before attacking.
  3. Keep your king safe.
  4. Think about your opponent's threats.
