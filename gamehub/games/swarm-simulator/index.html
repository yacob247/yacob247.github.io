About Swarm Simulator

Swarm Simulator is an idle game about growing and upgrading a swarm of insects. Starting with a single larva, you expand your hive, unlock new units, and push toward enormous numbers. It is a classic incremental experience with a satisfying progression curve.

How to play

Begin by producing larva and gathering meat. Spend resources to buy and upgrade units. Expand your territory and unlock new mechanics as you progress. The game continues offline, so you accumulate resources while away. Optimize your production and push toward new milestones.

Game modes

Single-player idle progression with offline gains.

Tips and tricks

  1. Focus on efficient upgrades early.
  2. Balance production and expansion.
  3. Check in regularly for offline rewards.
  4. Do not spread resources too thin.
