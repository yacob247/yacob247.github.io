About shapez.io

shapez.io is a factory-building automation game where you extract, refine, and combine shapes to meet production goals. With its minimalist visuals and deep systems, it is a relaxing yet satisfying game for anyone who loves automation and optimization.

How to play

Start by extracting basic shapes and build conveyor belts to transport them. Use machines to cut, rotate, paint, and combine shapes. Deliver the required shapes to complete levels and unlock new machines and mechanics. The game gets progressively more complex, requiring careful planning and efficient layouts. There is no time pressure — build at your own pace.

Game modes

Single-player factory-building with progressive levels and sandbox play.

Tips and tricks

  1. Plan your layouts before building.
  2. Keep belts organized and avoid crossing.
  3. Unlock new machines as soon as possible.
  4. Do not be afraid to rebuild sections for efficiency.
