About ZType

ZType is a typing shooter that turns your keyboard into a weapon. Type the words attached to incoming enemies to destroy them before they reach you. Fast, intense, and a brilliant way to practice typing speed under pressure.

How to play

Enemies approach with words attached. Type each word correctly to fire and destroy the enemy. The faster and more accurately you type, the better you survive. Waves get harder, with more and faster enemies. Your goal is to survive as long as possible and achieve a high score.

Game modes

Single-player arcade typing shooter with endless waves.

Tips and tricks

  1. Type accurately first — mistakes waste time.
  2. Prioritize the closest enemies.
  3. Keep your fingers on the home row.
  4. Use both hands and avoid looking at the keyboard.

Features

  • Endless waves with increasing difficulty.
