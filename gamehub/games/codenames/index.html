About Codenames

Codenames is the official online version of the hit word party game. Two teams compete to identify their secret agents using one-word clues from their spymaster. Clever clues, tense guessing, and the constant risk of hitting the assassin make every round unforgettable.

How to play

Split into two teams. Each team's spymaster sees a key showing which words belong to their team, the enemy team, neutral bystanders, and the assassin. Spymasters give one-word clues with a number, and their teammates guess words on the board. Guess a word belonging to your team and you continue; guess wrong and the turn passes. Find all your team's words first to win — but avoid the assassin.

Game modes

Team-based multiplayer word game with spymaster and guesser roles.

Tips and tricks

  1. Give clues that connect multiple words without being too vague.
  2. Avoid clues that could point to the assassin.
  3. As a guesser, discuss with your team before locking in.
  4. Track which words have been ruled out.
