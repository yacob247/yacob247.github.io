About Arras.io

Arras.io is a tank arena shooter that takes the classic formula and expands it with a huge variety of upgrade paths and playstyles. Choose your tank, level up, and branch into one of dozens of specialized forms, each with unique weapons and abilities. Fast, deep, and endlessly replayable, it is a must-try for fans of arena shooters.

How to play

Spawn as a basic tank and start shooting shapes and enemies to earn experience. Level up to unlock upgrade options, then choose a path that fits your style — snipers, brawlers, supporters, and more. Each upgrade changes how your tank plays. Fight other players and dominate the leaderboard. The arena is constantly active, so stay aware of your surroundings and pick your fights wisely.

Game modes

Multiplayer arena combat with extensive tank upgrade trees.

Tips and tricks

  1. Farm shapes early to level up quickly before engaging other players.
  2. Pick an upgrade path that suits your playstyle and stick with it.
  3. Do not fight above your level — upgraded tanks will destroy you.
  4. Stay near teammates in team modes and focus fire on key targets.
  5. Learn what each tank type does so you can counter it.
