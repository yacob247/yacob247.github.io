About Bonk.io

Bonk.io is pure, chaotic physics fun. You control a ball-like fighter and bash opponents off the arena in a game that is easy to learn and hilarious to master. With custom maps, private rooms, and a community full of creative game modes, it is the kind of game you play for a laugh and end up staying for hours.

How to play

Move your character around the arena and use momentum to knock other players into the void. Your weight and speed affect how hard you hit and how hard you get hit. Use walls and platforms to reposition, build up speed, and time your strikes. Push enemies off the edge while avoiding the same fate yourself. Custom maps and modes change the rules, so each room can offer something entirely different.

Game modes

Multiplayer physics fighting with public matches, private rooms, and custom maps and game modes.

Tips and tricks

  1. Build momentum before you strike — speed equals knockback power.
  2. Use walls to bounce off and reposition without losing control.
  3. Aim your hits toward the nearest edge of the arena.
  4. Stay near the center when you are heavier; force light opponents to the rim.
  5. Learn the map before fighting hard — edges and gaps are everywhere.

Features

  • Physics-based multiplayer fighting with knockback gameplay.
  • Private rooms so you can play with friends.
  • Custom maps and community-created modes.
  • Easy to learn but full of skill expression.
