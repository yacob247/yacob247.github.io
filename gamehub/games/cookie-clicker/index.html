About Cookie Clicker

Cookie Clicker is the legendary idle game that turned a single click into an obsession. You start by baking one cookie at a time, then build a sprawling empire of grandmas, farms, factories, and stranger things, all working to produce cookies while you are away. It is deceptively simple, wonderfully absurd, and dangerously hard to stop playing.

How to play

Click the big cookie to earn cookies. Spend those cookies on buildings and upgrades that produce cookies automatically over time. The more you invest, the faster your production grows. Unlock achievements, buy powerful upgrades, and watch your cookies-per-second climb into absurd numbers. The game continues generating cookies even while you are offline, so returning after a break means a huge payout. Prestige mechanics let you reset for permanent bonuses once you reach a certain point.

Game modes

Single-player idle progression with an offline production system and prestige resets.

Tips and tricks

  1. Buy the cheapest efficient upgrades early to snowball your production.
  2. Prioritize buildings with the best cookies-per-second-per-cost ratio.
  3. Check back regularly for golden cookies, which give strong temporary boosts.
  4. Do not ignore achievements — many grant permanent bonuses.
  5. Prestige once progress slows down; the permanent multiplier is worth the reset.
