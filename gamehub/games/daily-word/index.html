About Daily Word

Daily Word is a daily five-letter word puzzle where you have six tries to guess the secret word. With color-coded feedback guiding each guess, it is a fun and satisfying daily brain-teaser for word lovers.

How to play

Type a five-letter word and press Enter. Green tiles mean the letter is correct and in the right spot, yellow means correct letter but wrong spot, and gray means the letter is not in the word. Use the feedback to narrow down the answer. You have six guesses per day.

Game modes

Single-player daily word puzzle.

Tips and tricks

  1. Start with a word that covers common letters.
  2. Use your second guess to test new letters.
  3. Avoid repeating gray letters.
  4. Take your time — there is no timer.

Features

  • Daily five-letter word puzzle.
  • Color-coded feedback.
  • Six guesses per day.
  • Free to play in the browser.
