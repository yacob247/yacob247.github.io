About Sporcle

Sporcle is one of the largest trivia quiz platforms on the web, with millions of user-created quizzes across every topic imaginable. From geography and history to sports and pop culture, it is a trivia lover's paradise.

How to play

Browse or search for a quiz, then complete it within the time limit. Quizzes use typed answers, multiple choice, or clicking on maps and images. Scores and rankings let you compete with other players. Many quizzes are created by the community, so there is always something new.

Game modes

Single-player quizzes across a huge range of user-created and official content.

Tips and tricks

  1. Start with popular quizzes to see the format.
  2. Read quiz descriptions for clues about the format.
  3. Use the 'give up' option to learn answers.
  4. Try creating your own quiz for fun.

Features
