About Truck Simulator 1

Truck Simulator 1 puts you behind the wheel of a heavy haulage rig and hands you the keys to the highway. Deliver cargo across city streets and open roads, manage your speed through tight turns, and keep your load intact from pickup to drop-off. It is a relaxed but focused driving experience where patience, smooth control, and a good sense of your vehicle's size are what separate a clean delivery from a wrecked trailer.

How to play

Accept a delivery contract, then drive your truck along the assigned route to the destination. Steer carefully through traffic, obey the road where required, and avoid collisions that damage your cargo. The heavier your load, the slower you accelerate and the longer you need to brake, so plan corners and stops in advance. Complete deliveries to earn money and progress to bigger jobs, better trucks, and longer routes.

Game modes

Single-player delivery campaign with free driving on city and highway routes.

Tips and tricks

  1. Brake early — a loaded trailer takes far longer to stop than a car.
  2. Take corners wide; cutting them risks clipping traffic or the curb.
  3. Watch your mirrors when changing lanes, especially with a long trailer.
  4. Smooth, gentle steering prevents jackknifing and keeps cargo safe.
  5. Do not chase speed — a clean delivery always beats a fast crash.

Features

  • City and highway routes with traffic to navigate.
  • Progression through increasingly demanding jobs.
  • Runs smoothly in the browser via HTML5.
  • Playable on desktop and mobile.

Frequently asked questions

What is Truck Simulator 1?

It is a browser-based truck driving simulation where you haul cargo along city and highway routes and try to deliver it without damage.

Is it difficult to control the truck?

It is easy to learn but takes practice — the truck is heavy and slow to stop, so smooth steering and early braking matter.

Does it need a download?

No, it runs directly in your browser as an HTML5 game.
