About Warzone

Warzone is a turn-based multiplayer strategy game of world conquest. Build armies, forge alliances, and outmaneuver opponents across detailed maps in a game that rewards planning, diplomacy, and patience. Every move matters, and every territory you take is a step closer to global dominance.

How to play

Choose a map, then take turns deploying armies and attacking neighboring territories. Use dice-based combat to resolve battles. Expand your control while defending your borders. Form alliances and negotiate with other players. The last player standing or the one who completes the map's objective wins.

Game modes

Turn-based multiplayer strategy with many maps and customizable rules.

Tips and tricks

  1. Secure continents for bonus armies.
  2. Do not overextend — defend your borders.
  3. Use diplomacy to avoid fighting on multiple fronts.
  4. Plan several turns ahead before committing to a big attack.

Features

  • Turn-based multiplayer strategy.

  • Diplomacy and alliance systems.
