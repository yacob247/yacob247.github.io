About Brutal.io

Brutal.io is a physics-based arena fighter where momentum, timing, and a good sense of humor all play a role. Knock opponents off platforms, slam into them at speed, and use the environment itself as a weapon. Fast, chaotic, and endlessly replayable, it is a game that turns simple movement into deadly strategy.

How to play

Move your fighter around the arena using momentum and physics. Slam into opponents to knock them back and push them toward edges or hazards. Use platforms and terrain to build speed and set up angles. The last player or team standing wins, so avoid being near ledges when you are vulnerable. Timing your strikes is everything — a well-aimed slam can flip a fight in an instant.

Game modes

Multiplayer arena combat with physics-driven knockback and elimination gameplay.

Tips and tricks

  1. Build speed before you slam — momentum is your main weapon.
  2. Fight in the center of the arena, not near edges, unless you are going for a kill.
  3. Bait opponents toward ledges and dodge at the last second.
  4. Watch your own position; accidental self-eliminations are common.
  5. Use the arena's geometry to redirect your charge into a target.
