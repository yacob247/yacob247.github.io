About TETR.IO

TETR.IO is a modern, competitive take on the falling-block puzzle genre, built for players who want precision, speed, and a real ranking system. With buttery-smooth controls, a clean interface, and deep mechanical skill ceilings, it is the definitive way to play Tetris-style games in your browser. Casual players can enjoy endless stacking, while competitive players can climb the ladder.

How to play

Blocks fall from the top of the screen and you rotate and position them to form complete lines, which then clear. Clearing multiple lines at once and chaining clears builds score and sends garbage to opponents in versus modes. Learn the hold and next-piece previews, use hard drops to move quickly, and manage your stack height. In ranked play, you face other players in real time, sending attacks and surviving their garbage. The goal is simple: survive, clear lines, and outlast your opponent.

Game modes

Solo play, ranked multiplayer versus, and custom lobbies with adjustable rules.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep your stack flat — uneven stacks limit your options fast.
  2. Use the hold feature to save a piece you cannot place well.
  3. Practice hard drops and rotations until they are muscle memory.
  4. In versus, build up a large attack and send it all at once.
  5. Learn to play from both previews so you are never surprised by the next piece.
