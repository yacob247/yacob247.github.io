About Sky Hopper

Sky Hopper is an endless jumper game where you bounce upward on drifting platforms. Keep climbing higher and higher while avoiding gaps and hazards. Simple, addictive, and perfect for quick sessions.

How to play

Your character bounces automatically. Steer left and right to land on platforms. If you miss a platform, you fall and the game ends. Platforms drift and disappear as you climb, so keep moving upward. Chase your highest score.

Game modes

Single-player endless jumper with score chasing.

Tips and tricks

  1. Look ahead for the next platform.
  2. Do not overcorrect — small movements are safer.
  3. Stay near the center of platforms.
  4. Practice predicting platform drift.

Features

  • Endless platform jumper.
  • Simple controls with increasing challenge.
  • High-score chasing.
  • Free to play in the browser.
