About Venge.io

Venge.io is a sharp, competitive browser FPS built for players who love tactical team play. Fast movement, tight gunplay, and distinct character abilities combine into matches that reward coordination and quick decision-making. Every round is a chance to outthink your opponents, control key positions, and land the shots that swing the fight.

How to play

Join a team match and pick a character with unique abilities. Work with your teammates to control the map, secure objectives, and eliminate the enemy team. Use your abilities to gain an edge — whether that is mobility, area denial, or direct damage — and combine them with your weapon fire. Reload behind cover, communicate your pushes, and adapt to how the enemy team is playing. Winning comes from controlling space as a group rather than hunting for solo kills.

Game modes

Team-based competitive matches with objective and elimination-style play, featuring multiple characters with unique abilities.

Tips and tricks

  1. Move with your team — solo pushes rarely survive against grouped defenders.
  2. Save your character ability for the moment it swings a fight, not for random use.
  3. Hold angles and let enemies walk into your crosshair rather than chasing kills.
  4. Reload before you push, not after you are already committed.
  5. Watch which lanes the enemy favors and shift your team to counter them.
