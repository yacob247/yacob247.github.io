About Wormate.io

Wormate.io takes the classic snake formula and turns it into a colorful, competitive multiplayer feast. Grow your worm by eating candy and sweets, cut off opponents to make them drop their food, and fight your way up the leaderboard. Simple to control, hard to master, and endlessly addictive in short bursts.

How to play

Guide your worm around the map eating candy and food to grow longer. The longer you get, the higher you climb on the leaderboard — but also the easier you are to trap. Use speed boosts to close in on prey or escape danger, but boosting drains your length. Cut off other worms so they crash into you and drop their food, then scoop it up. Avoid head-on collisions and keep moving to survive.

Game modes

Multiplayer free-for-all arcade mode with a live leaderboard.

Tips and tricks

  1. Only boost when you are sure of the payoff — it costs you length.
  2. Circle smaller worms to trap them against your body.
  3. Stay near the map edges less often; you need room to escape.
  4. Eat dense candy clusters to grow fast instead of chasing single pieces.
  5. Do not get greedy near bigger worms — one wrong turn ends your run.

Features

  • Multiplayer snake-style arcade gameplay.
  • A live leaderboard that makes every run competitive.
  • Colorful candy-themed maps and skins.
  • Playable on desktop and mobile browsers.
  • Quick sessions that are easy to pick up and put down.
