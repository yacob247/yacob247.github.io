About Pinball — Neon Table

Pinball — Neon Table is a classic pinball game with a vibrant neon aesthetic. Work the flippers, keep the ball alive, and rack up a high score. With its satisfying physics and bright visuals, it is a great browser arcade experience.

How to play

Use the flippers to hit the ball and score points. Keep the ball in play as long as possible and aim for high-value targets. Different bumpers and ramps trigger bonuses. Losing the ball costs you a life. You typically have three balls to score as high as possible.

Game modes

Single-player pinball with score chasing.

Tips and tricks

  1. Use small flipper taps for fine control.
  2. Aim for high-value targets.
  3. Keep the ball away from the drain.
  4. Watch the ball, not the score.

Features
