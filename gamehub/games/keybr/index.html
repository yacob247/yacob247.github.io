About Keybr

Keybr is a smart typing tutor that adapts to your weaknesses. It generates personalized lessons based on the keys you struggle with, helping you improve efficiently. With a clean interface and data-driven approach, it is one of the best tools for learning to type faster.

How to play

Start a lesson and type the generated text. Keybr tracks your speed and accuracy per key, then adjusts future lessons to focus on your weak spots. As you improve, new keys and more complex text are introduced. The system adapts continuously, so every session is tailored to you.

Game modes

Adaptive typing lessons with personalized key practice.

Tips and tricks

  1. Practice a little every day rather than long sessions occasionally.
  2. Focus on accuracy over raw speed.
  3. Do not look at the keyboard — trust the process.
  4. Review your stats to see which keys need work.
