About Kittens Game

Kittens Game is a deep, text-based civilization builder that hides remarkable complexity beneath a simple interface. Starting with a single kitten gathering catnip, you gradually develop a society through science, industry, and trade, expanding from a humble village into a sprawling civilization. It is a slow-burn idle game for players who love resource chains, long-term planning, and the satisfaction of watching numbers grow.

How to play

Assign your kittens to jobs like farming, woodcutting, mining, and science, and build structures that unlock new resources and improvements. Research technologies to advance your civilization, manage happiness and storage limits, and balance production chains. Progress is persistent and continues while you are away, so the game rewards planning rather than constant clicking. Over time you unlock seasons, trade, religion, and eventually resets that let you start over with permanent bonuses.

Game modes

Single-player idle civilization building with persistent offline progression and long-term resets.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep happiness high — it boosts almost all production.
  2. Balance your jobs; too many kittens on one resource starves the others.
  3. Prioritize storage upgrades before big production pushes.
  4. Unlock new technologies as soon as you can afford them; they compound.
  5. Be patient — Kittens Game is a marathon, not a sprint.

Features

  • Deep civilization-building idle game with complex resource chains.
  • Technology research, trade, religion, and seasonal mechanics.
  • Persistent progression that continues offline.
  • Long-term reset and prestige systems.
  • Runs entirely in the browser.
