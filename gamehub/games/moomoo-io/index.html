About MooMoo.io

MooMoo.io is a survival combat game where you gather resources, build a base, and fight other players in a top-down arena. Fast-paced and competitive, it rewards quick decisions and smart resource management. Build defenses, craft weapons, and take on other players in a constant battle for survival.

How to play

Gather wood, stone, and food to survive and build. Construct walls, spikes, and other defenses around your base. Craft weapons and tools to fight other players. Upgrade your character and structures as you gather more resources. Fight for control of the map and be the last player or team standing. Resource management and base design are just as important as combat skill.

Game modes

Multiplayer survival combat with base building and PvP.

Tips and tricks

  1. Build walls early to protect your resources.
  2. Keep your base compact and well-defended rather than sprawling.
  3. Upgrade your weapons before attacking other players.
  4. Gather food consistently — you need it to heal.
  5. Scout enemy bases before raiding; some are better defended than they look.

Features

  • PvP multiplayer with base raiding.
