About Survev.io

Survev.io is a top-down battle royale survival game in the revival style. Drop in, loot up, and fight to be the last player standing while the safe zone shrinks around you. With its fast pace and straightforward mechanics, it is a great browser battle royale for quick, competitive sessions.

How to play

Drop onto the map and scavenge for weapons, ammunition, and healing items. Fight other players and avoid the shrinking safe zone. Use cover and positioning to gain an advantage. Manage your inventory carefully and stay stocked on healing. Be the last player or team standing to win. Matches are fast, so every decision counts.

Game modes

Top-down battle royale with solo and team modes.

Tips and tricks

  1. Loot up before engaging other players.
  2. Stay near the edge of the safe zone to avoid being surrounded.
  3. Keep healing items in your inventory — they decide close fights.
  4. Use trees and buildings for cover.
  5. Rotate early so you are never caught in the storm.
