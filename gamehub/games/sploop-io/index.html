About Sploop.io

Sploop.io is a survival combat game where you gather, build, and fight in a persistent multiplayer world. Start with nothing, chop trees, mine stone, and craft tools and weapons to defend yourself against other players and the environment. It blends the grind of survival crafting with the constant threat of PvP, creating a tense and rewarding experience.

How to play

Begin by gathering basic resources like wood and stone, then use them to craft tools, weapons, and buildings. Build a base to protect your resources and yourself. Hunt animals for food and fight other players for control of territory. Upgrade your gear over time to take on tougher challenges. The world is competitive, so always stay alert for attackers.

Game modes

Multiplayer survival combat with crafting, building, and PvP.

Tips and tricks

  1. Build a small base early to protect your resources before you start expanding.
  2. Keep a weapon on you at all times; PvP attacks come without warning.
  3. Do not over-invest in a base near other players; location matters.
  4. Stockpile food and healing items before any long excursion.
  5. Ally with other players if possible — solo survival is tough.
