About Tetris

Tetris is the timeless puzzle game that defined a genre. Rotate and drop falling blocks to complete lines and clear them before the stack reaches the top. Simple, addictive, and endlessly satisfying, it remains one of the most beloved games of all time.

How to play

Blocks of different shapes fall from the top. Move and rotate them to fit together and complete horizontal lines. Completed lines disappear and give you points. The game speeds up as you progress. If the blocks reach the top, it is game over. Try to clear as many lines as possible and beat your high score.

Game modes

Single-player classic Tetris with score chasing.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep the stack flat — avoid deep holes.
  2. Save the straight piece for a Tetris (four lines at once).
  3. Plan ahead for the next pieces.
  4. Clear lines regularly to avoid a tall stack.
