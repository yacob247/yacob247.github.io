About Surviv.io

Surviv.io is the original top-down battle royale that proved a browser game could deliver real tension. Drop onto a huge map, loot weapons and armor, and fight to be the last person standing while the deadly red zone closes in. Simple to read and brutally competitive, it is a game of positioning, resource management, and nerves.

How to play

You start with nothing and must scavenge weapons, ammunition, healing items, and armor from buildings and crates scattered across the map. The safe zone shrinks over time, and standing in the red area drains your health fast. Fight other players, use cover, and manage your inventory carefully. Play solo, or team up in duos and squads where reviving teammates becomes part of the strategy. Survive to the final circle and win.

Game modes

Solo, duo, and squad battle royale with shrinking safe zones.

Tips and tricks

  1. Always keep healing items in your inventory — they decide close fights.
  2. Rotate early so you are never forced to run through the red zone.
  3. Use trees, rocks, and buildings as cover; open ground is death.
  4. Squad up so a downed teammate can be revived instead of eliminated.
  5. Swap to a shotgun in close-quarters buildings and a rifle in the open.

Features

  • Top-down battle royale with solo, duo, and squad modes.
  • A large map full of lootable buildings and cover.
  • A shrinking red zone that forces constant movement.
  • Deep inventory and healing management.
  • Instant browser play with no download.
