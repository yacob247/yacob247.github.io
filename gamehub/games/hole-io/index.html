About Hole.io

Hole.io is a delightfully chaotic arcade game where you play as a hungry hole in the ground. Start small, swallow everything you can fit, and grow large enough to consume entire buildings. With other holes competing for the same city, it becomes a race to eat the most and dominate the map before time runs out.

How to play

Move your hole around the city and swallow objects smaller than you. Start with benches, cones, and trash cans, then work your way up to cars, trees, and eventually whole buildings as you grow. Other players control their own holes and will compete for the same objects — and can even swallow you if you are much smaller. Grow as fast as possible before the timer ends, and finish with the highest score.

Game modes

Multiplayer arcade free-for-all with timed matches and a score leaderboard.

Tips and tricks

  1. Prioritize dense clusters of small objects early to grow quickly.
  2. Avoid bigger holes until you have grown enough to compete.
  3. Move to areas with large buildings once you can swallow them.
  4. Do not linger in crowded spots where other holes are eating everything.
  5. Keep moving constantly — standing still wastes growth time.

Features

  • Arcade gameplay where you swallow a whole city.
  • Real-time competition against other player-controlled holes.
  • Timed matches with a score leaderboard.
  • Playable on desktop and mobile browsers.
  • Satisfying growth progression from tiny to gigantic.

Frequently asked questions

Hole.io is a multiplayer arcade game where you control a hole in the ground and swallow objects to grow bigger while competing against other holes.

Yes — a much larger hole can swallow you, so it pays to avoid bigger opponents until you have grown.
