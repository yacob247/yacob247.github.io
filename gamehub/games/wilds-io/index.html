About Wilds.io

Wilds.io is a multiplayer survival and combat game set in a wild, untamed world. Gather resources, craft weapons and tools, and fight other players in a brutal environment where only the prepared survive. It blends crafting, exploration, and PvP into a fast, competitive experience.

How to play

Spawn into the world and start gathering resources like wood, stone, and food. Craft weapons, tools, and armor to survive. Build shelters and fires to stay safe. Other players are a constant threat, so always be ready for combat. Hunt, gather, explore, and fight your way to dominance. The more you gather and craft, the stronger you become.

Game modes

Multiplayer survival combat with crafting, building, and PvP.

Tips and tricks

  1. Gather food first — hunger kills as surely as other players.
  2. Craft a basic weapon before exploring far from your spawn.
  3. Build a small shelter to protect yourself during vulnerable moments.
  4. Avoid fights you cannot win; retreating is a valid strategy.
  5. Upgrade your gear as soon as you can afford it.

Features
