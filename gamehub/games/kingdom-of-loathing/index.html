About Kingdom of Loathing

Kingdom of Loathing is a humorous browser RPG full of clever writing, absurd monsters, and turn-based adventures. Explore a strange world, complete quests, and level up your character in a game that is as funny as it is deep. It is a classic browser RPG with a loyal community.

Create a character and explore the Kingdom. Complete quests, fight monsters in turn-based combat, and level up. Choose a class with unique abilities and playstyles. Collect items, cook food, and mix cocktails. The game is packed with secrets and jokes for players who explore.
