About Voxiom

Voxiom is a voxel battle royale where building and shooting are equally important. The world is made of blocks, and so are your defenses — mine materials, construct cover, and turn the terrain into a weapon as the safe zone closes in. With fast fights and total freedom to reshape the battlefield, no two matches play out the same way.

How to play

Land on the map and gather weapons, resources, and building blocks. Mine the environment to collect materials, then place blocks to build walls, ramps, and towers for protection and high ground. The storm shrinks the playable area, forcing players together. Eliminate opponents and stay alive until you are the last one standing. Because building is instant, fights often become rapid constructions and counter-constructions, so flexibility matters as much as aim.

Game modes

Voxel battle royale with solo and squad-style play, focused on building and shooting.

Tips and tricks

  1. Mine resources whenever you have downtime — materials win late-game fights.
  2. Build a quick wall the instant you take fire to break your opponent's aim.
  3. Use ramps to gain height, then shoot down at enemies below.
  4. Do not build a huge base in the open — it paints a target on you.
  5. Rotate toward the zone early so you are not caught in the storm.

Features

  • Full building system for instant cover and high ground.
  • Multiple weapons and resources to collect.
  • Free to play in the browser.
  • Fast, replayable matches with shifting battlefields.
