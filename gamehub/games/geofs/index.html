About GeoFS

GeoFS is a remarkably detailed flight simulator that runs entirely in your browser, letting you take to the skies over real-world scenery. Choose from a range of aircraft, from light prop planes to commercial jets, and fly anywhere on the globe. With realistic flight dynamics and satellite-based terrain, it is an accessible but genuine taste of aviation.

How to play

Select an aircraft and an airport, then take off using the throttle and controls. Manage your speed, pitch, and heading to climb, cruise, and descend safely. The instruments give you altitude, speed, and navigation information, and the world below is mapped from real satellite imagery. Practice landings carefully — approach too fast or too steep and you will crash. Longer flights let you navigate between airports and explore different regions.

Game modes

Free flight simulation with multiple aircraft and global scenery, plus multiplayer where available.

Tips and tricks

  1. Climb gradually at a steady speed rather than pulling back hard.
  2. Reduce throttle and descend gently on approach to avoid overshooting the runway.
  3. Watch your airspeed indicator — stalling is the most common beginner mistake.
  4. Trim your aircraft so you are not constantly fighting the controls.
  5. Practice takeoffs and landings at a single airport before flying long routes.

Features

  • Free browser-based flight simulator with global satellite scenery.
  • A range of aircraft from light planes to airliners.
  • Realistic flight dynamics and instrument readouts.
  • Multiplayer flying in supported sessions.
  • No installation — fly directly from your browser.

Frequently asked questions

What is GeoFS?

GeoFS is a free browser flight simulator that lets you fly various aircraft over real-world satellite-mapped scenery.

Is GeoFS realistic?

It offers accessible but genuine flight dynamics, including stall behavior and instrument-based navigation, though it is easier than a full desktop simulator.

Can I fly anywhere in the world?

Yes, the entire globe is available to explore, and you can take off from and land at many airports.
