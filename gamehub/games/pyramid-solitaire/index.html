About Pyramid Solitaire

Pyramid Solitaire is a classic card game where you pair cards that add to 13 to dismantle a pyramid. With its simple rules and satisfying progression, it is a relaxing solitaire variant for card game fans.

How to play

The pyramid is made of cards stacked in rows. You can remove pairs of exposed cards that add up to 13 (aces count as 1, jacks as 11, queens as 12, kings as 13 and can be removed alone). You can also draw from the deck. Clear the entire pyramid to win.

Game modes

Single-player solitaire with score tracking.

Tips and tricks

  1. Prioritize cards that block important rows.
  2. Use the deck wisely — it is limited.
  3. Track which cards remain.
  4. Kings can be removed alone, so save them for blockers.
