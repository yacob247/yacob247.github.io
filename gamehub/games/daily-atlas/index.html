About Daily Atlas

Daily Atlas is a daily geography puzzle where you guess the country of the day in six tries. Each guess gives you feedback on how close you are, turning world geography into a rewarding daily habit. Perfect for map lovers and trivia fans.

How to play

Each day, a new mystery country is chosen. Type your guesses and see how close you are. Feedback includes direction, distance, and other hints to narrow down the location. You have six tries to guess correctly. A new country appears each day.

Game modes

Single-player daily country-guessing puzzle.

Tips and tricks

  1. Start with well-known countries to get directional hints.
  2. Use the distance and direction feedback carefully.
  3. Learn small countries to improve.
  4. Do not waste guesses on unlikely options.

Features

  • Daily country-guessing puzzle.
  • Distance and direction hints.
  • Six tries per puzzle.
  • Free to play in the browser.
