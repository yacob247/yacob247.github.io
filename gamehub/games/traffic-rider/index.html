About Traffic Rider

Traffic Rider puts you on a motorcycle and dares you to carve through packed highways at terrifying speed. The rush of weaving between cars, the roar of the engine, and the constant threat of a crash make every run a test of nerve and precision. With dozens of bikes to upgrade and missions that push your reflexes, this is arcade racing at its most adrenaline-fueled.

How to play

You ride forward automatically while you steer around traffic, using near misses and speed to earn rewards. Complete mission objectives — such as reaching a distance, overtaking a number of vehicles, or finishing a route without crashing — to earn cash. Spend that cash on new bikes or upgrades to improve speed, handling, and braking. Crashes end your run or cost you time, so reading traffic flow and choosing the right gaps is everything. The faster you ride, the more you earn, but the less room you have for error.

Game modes

Single-player career-style missions and endless run gameplay, with first-person and third-person motorcycle control.

Tips and tricks

  1. Look far ahead down the road, not at the car directly in front of you.
  2. Weave smoothly — sudden swerves at high speed are how runs end.
  3. Near misses earn extra rewards, so skim close when it is safe.
  4. Upgrade handling and brakes before raw top speed early on.
  5. Save up for a better bike rather than repeatedly repairing a weak starter model.

Features

  • First-person and third-person motorcycle racing with realistic traffic.
  • Over 30 bikes to unlock and upgrade.
  • Mission-based progression alongside endless high-score runs.
  • Beautiful 3D highways and city scenery.
  • Plays instantly in the browser on desktop and mobile.

Frequently asked questions

What is Traffic Rider?

Traffic Rider is a first-person motorcycle racing game where you dodge traffic, complete missions, and upgrade a garage of bikes.

How do I earn money in Traffic Rider?

You earn cash by completing missions, hitting speed and distance goals, and pulling off near misses with traffic.

Can I play Traffic Rider on my phone?

Yes, the game supports touch controls and works on mobile browsers as well as desktop.
