About Little Alchemy 2

Little Alchemy 2 is a charming puzzle sandbox where the entire world is built from combinations. Start with four basic elements and combine them to discover everything from fire and water to life, myth, and beyond. With hundreds of items to find and a relaxing, curiosity-driven pace, it is the kind of game that quietly eats an afternoon.

How to play

Drag elements from your sidebar onto the workspace and drop one on top of another to combine them. If the combination is valid, a new element appears and is added to your collection. Start with simple pairs like water and fire to make steam, then work outward into more complex discoveries. The goal is to find every element, and the game offers hints and categories to help when you get stuck. There is no timer or failure — just experimentation.

Game modes

Single-player puzzle sandbox with free experimentation and a full element collection to complete.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep commonly used base elements like water and air easily accessible.
  2. Write down or remember combinations you have already tried.
  3. Think logically — most results follow real-world relationships.
  4. Use hints when you are stuck rather than giving up on a branch.
  5. Explore categories like life, myth, and technology for hidden chains.
