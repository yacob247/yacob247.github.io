About War Brokers

War Brokers is a browser FPS that goes bigger than most, adding tanks, jeeps, and combined-arms warfare to the mix. Infantry firefights, vehicle duels, and coordinated pushes across open maps create battles that feel large and unpredictable. If you want a shooter where a well-driven tank can turn the tide, this is the one.

How to play

Join a match and choose how you want to fight — on foot with a rifle, behind the wheel of a jeep, or commanding a tank. Infantry capture objectives, hold positions, and support vehicles; vehicles break fortified positions and dominate open ground. Work as a squad, coordinate your pushes, and use the terrain for cover. The team that controls the battlefield and the objectives wins, so balance your forces between infantry and armor.

Game modes

Multiplayer team battles with combined infantry and vehicle combat across multiple maps.

Tips and tricks

  1. Do not fight tanks in the open as infantry — flank them or use cover.
  2. Keep a repair option handy if you are driving; vehicles under fire break down.
  3. Use vehicles to break stalemates but support them with infantry.
  4. Communicate before pushing an objective so your team moves together.
  5. Learn the map's vehicle spawns to control the armor early.

Features

  • Combined infantry and vehicle warfare in the browser.
  • Tanks, jeeps, and other vehicles to pilot.
  • Multiple maps with objective-based team combat.
  • Large-scale battles with plenty of strategic depth.
