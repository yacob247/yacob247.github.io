About Tanki Online

Tanki Online is a long-running multiplayer tank shooter that has stayed popular for a reason — it is deep, accessible, and built for competitive play. Command a huge roster of tanks, customize your turret and hull, and battle across varied maps with teammates who rely on you. Whether you are pushing a flag or defending a point, every match is a proper tactical firefight.

How to play

Pick a tank combination — turret, hull, and protection — then join a match. Each combination plays differently: some turrets are long-range snipers, others are close-range brawlers, and some support the team with healing or area control. Drive, shoot, and coordinate with teammates to complete objectives. Earn experience and crystals to unlock better equipment. Communication and teamwork decide most matches.

Game modes

Multiple team modes including capture the flag, control points, team deathmatch, and more.

Tips and tricks

  1. Learn what each turret does so you can counter it before it kills you.
  2. Do not drive into open ground without support from teammates.
  3. Protect your flag carrier or hold the point — objectives win matches.
  4. Upgrade your favorite combination rather than spreading resources thin.
  5. Use hull abilities (like healing or speed) at the right moments, not on cooldown.

Features

  • Large roster of tanks with customizable turrets and hulls.
  • Multiple team modes and battle maps.
  • Deep upgrade and progression system.
  • Mature competitive community and matchmaking.
  • Plays directly in the browser.
