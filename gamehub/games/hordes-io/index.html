About Hordes.io

Hordes.io is a browser-based MMO that brings open-world combat, factions, and character progression to your browser tab. Choose your faction, pick a class, and fight for territory and glory in a persistent online world. With PvP, PvE, and faction warfare, it offers real MMO depth without the download.

How to play

Create a character, choose your faction and class, then enter the world. Complete quests, defeat monsters, and level up your character to unlock new abilities and gear. Join other players in faction battles and PvP zones to fight for control of territory. Group up for dungeons and tougher enemies. The world is persistent, so your progress carries between sessions.

Game modes

Persistent MMO world with PvE questing, PvP combat, and faction warfare.

Tips and tricks

  1. Complete early quests to level up quickly and unlock abilities.
  2. Join a group for tougher content; solo play is limited at higher levels.
  3. Learn your class's role — tank, healer, or damage — and play to it.
  4. Participate in faction battles for rewards and experience.
  5. Upgrade gear regularly; outdated equipment holds you back.
