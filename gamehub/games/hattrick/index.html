About Hattrick

Hattrick is one of the longest-running online football management games, and for good reason. Manage a club, develop youth players, and battle through leagues and cups against managers worldwide. With its deep training systems and real-time matches, it is a timeless experience for football strategists.

How to play

Take control of a club and manage everything from lineups to youth development. Train players in specific skills, buy and sell on the transfer market, and set tactics for each match. Matches play out in real time with detailed commentary. Compete in leagues and cups for trophies and promotion.

Game modes

Persistent online football management with leagues, cups, and player development.

Tips and tricks

  1. Invest in youth training for long-term gains.
  2. Study the transfer market for bargains.
  3. Adapt tactics to your opponent's strengths and weaknesses.
  4. Patience pays off — Hattrick is a marathon.
