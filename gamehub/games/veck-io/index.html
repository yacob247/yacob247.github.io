About Veck.io

Veck.io is a fast browser FPS with room-code matches and smooth, competitive shooting. Jump into quick firefights, invite friends with a code, and prove your aim across tight, action-packed arenas. It is pure, no-fuss shooter fun that loads instantly and keeps the pace high from the first shot.

How to play

Join a match or create a room with a code to play with friends. Pick your loadout and spawn into the arena. Move fast, aim true, and eliminate opponents while using cover and angles to stay alive. Matches are short, so every kill matters. Reload behind cover, communicate with your team, and adjust your weapon choice to the map and range of each fight.

Game modes

Room-code based multiplayer matches for quick competitive or friendly games.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep your crosshair at head height around corners.
  2. Strafe while shooting to make yourself harder to hit.
  3. Reload immediately after every fight.
  4. Learn the map's main lanes and pick angles that favor your weapon.
