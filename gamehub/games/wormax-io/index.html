About Wormax.io

Wormax.io is a multiplayer snake arena game with boosts, skins, and fast-paced competition. Grow your worm by eating food, use boosts to outmaneuver opponents, and climb the leaderboard. It is easy to pick up, hard to master, and perfect for quick, competitive sessions.

How to play

Control your worm and eat food to grow longer. Use boosts to move faster and cut off opponents, but be careful — boosting costs you length. Avoid crashing into other worms, and try to make them crash into you. The longer you get, the higher your score, but the harder you are to control. Stay mobile and look for opportunities to trap opponents.

Game modes

Multiplayer snake arena with leaderboard competition.

Tips and tricks

  1. Only boost when you are sure of the payoff — it costs you length.
  2. Circle smaller worms to trap them.
  3. Stay away from the edges when possible.
  4. Do not chase players who are clearly trying to bait you.
  5. Keep your worm moving smoothly to avoid accidental crashes.
