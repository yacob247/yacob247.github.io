About Devast.io

Devast.io is a survival game with a focus on crafting, building, and the constant danger of other players. In a hostile world, you must gather resources, build a base, and craft weapons and tools to stay alive. It is a tense, competitive experience where every decision — where to build, what to craft, who to trust — matters.

How to play

Start by gathering basic resources and crafting simple tools. Build a base to protect yourself and your supplies. Craft better weapons, armor, and structures as you gather more materials. Other players will try to raid your base or ambush you in the wild, so always be prepared. Explore the world for rare resources and fight for survival. The strongest and smartest players dominate.

Game modes

Multiplayer survival with crafting, building, and PvP.

Tips and tricks

  1. Build your base away from high-traffic areas to avoid early raids.
  2. Never leave your base undefended when you go out gathering.
  3. Craft a decent weapon early — you will need it.
  4. Keep a hidden stash of emergency supplies.
  5. Scout other bases before attacking; some are better defended than they look.
