About RuneScape

RuneScape is one of the most iconic browser MMORPGs of all time. Explore a vast fantasy world, complete quests, level up dozens of skills, and interact with a massive community. It is a timeless adventure that has captivated millions of players for decades.

How to play

Create a character and enter the world of Gielinor. Complete quests, fight monsters, and train skills like combat, mining, fishing, and crafting. The game is open-ended — you can focus on whatever interests you. Join a clan, trade with other players, and explore the huge map. Progression is deep and rewarding.

Game modes

Persistent online MMORPG with quests, skills, and multiplayer interaction.

Tips and tricks

  1. Do quests early — they unlock useful rewards.
  2. Train multiple skills to keep the game fresh.
  3. Join a clan for social play and support.
  4. Use the wiki for guidance on complex content.

Features
