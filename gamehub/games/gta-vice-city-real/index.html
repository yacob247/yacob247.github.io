About GTA Vice City REAL

Step into the neon-soaked streets of Vice City, a sun-drenched metropolis ruled by fast cars, glamorous nightlife, and ruthless criminals. This open-world action-adventure classic puts you in the shoes of a rising hustler determined to climb to the top of the underworld. With a sprawling city to explore, a gripping story, and a soundtrack that defines an era, every drive and every shootout feels like a scene from a blockbuster crime film.

How to play

You start as a small-time operator and work your way up by completing story missions, side jobs, and criminal enterprises scattered across Vice City. Travel the open world on foot or by vehicle, switch between weapons, and take on rival gangs, corrupt officials, and law enforcement. Missions range from high-speed chases and delivery runs to full-scale shootouts and property takeovers. Earn cash, buy safehouses, upgrade your arsenal, and unlock new districts as your reputation grows. Freedom is the point — you can follow the story or simply cruise the coast and cause chaos.

Game modes

Single-player open-world campaign with free-roam exploration. Story missions, side activities, and sandbox play can be tackled in any order you like.

Tips and tricks

  1. Learn the city layout early — shortcuts save you during timed chases.
  2. Keep a fast, durable vehicle near your safehouse for emergencies.
  3. Stock up on health and armor before starting any mission that involves gunfire.
  4. Use cover and aim for the head during shootouts — rushing open ground gets you killed.
  5. Save often between missions so a failed attempt never costs you real progress.
