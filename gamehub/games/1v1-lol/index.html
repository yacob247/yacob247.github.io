About 1v1.LOL

1v1.LOL is a lightning-fast browser shooter that blends sharp gunplay with on-the-fly building, turning every duel into a test of reflexes and creativity. Whether you are throwing up walls to block incoming fire or editing your structure to gain the perfect angle, each match feels like a miniature chess game played at full speed. It is Fortnite-style combat distilled into pure, competitive 1v1 action.

How to play

Enter a match and immediately begin gathering materials and preparing for combat. Shoot opponents with your weapon while building walls, ramps, and platforms to protect yourself and gain height. Switch between weapon and build modes instantly — the faster you build, the harder you are to hit. Win duels by eliminating your opponent before they eliminate you. Practice your build-fight combos; the best players chain shots and structures seamlessly.

Game modes

1v1 duels, plus practice and free-build modes to sharpen your mechanics.

Tips and tricks

  1. Always build a wall before peeking — never take an open fight.
  2. Learn a few reliable ramp-rush patterns and drill them until they are muscle memory.
  3. Use edits to create unexpected angles instead of firing through the same window.
  4. Keep calm during build fights; panic building wastes materials and exposes you.
  5. Aim training matters as much as building — a good shot ends fights faster than a fancy tower.
