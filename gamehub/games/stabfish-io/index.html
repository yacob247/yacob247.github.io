About Stabfish.io

Stabfish.io takes the arena combat formula underwater, where fish wield weapons and fight for survival in a chaotic ocean brawl. Fast movement, sharp weapons, and a whole lot of stabbing make each match a frantic scramble for dominance. It is silly, fast, and surprisingly skill-based.

How to play

Control your fish and swim around the arena looking for fights. Use your weapon to stab other fish, and avoid getting hit yourself. Defeat enemies to grow stronger and unlock upgrades. The arena is full of hazards and other players, so positioning and timing matter. Stay nimble — the fish that stops moving is the fish that gets stabbed.

Game modes

Multiplayer arena combat with fish, weapons, and upgrades.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep circling and keep moving — stationary fish get picked off fast.
  2. Time your stabs for when enemies are committed to their own attacks.
  3. Avoid getting surrounded; escape to open water when outnumbered.
  4. Upgrade your weapon speed early to win more close fights.
  5. Watch for ambushes from behind — always know what is around you.
