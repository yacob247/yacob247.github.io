About HaxBall

HaxBall is a physics-based multiplayer football game that has been a browser classic for years. Simple to learn but full of skill expression, it turns a small ball and two goals into a surprisingly competitive sport. Whether you are playing a casual pickup game or a serious league match, the physics-driven gameplay keeps every match unpredictable and fun.

How to play

Control a circular player on a small pitch and try to kick the ball into the opponent's goal. Use your body to block, tackle, and pass. The ball and players have momentum, so positioning and timing are everything. Work with teammates to create chances and defend your goal. Matches are fast and goals can come from anywhere, so stay alert at all times.

Game modes

Multiplayer football matches with team play and custom rooms.

Tips and tricks

  1. Position yourself between the ball and your goal when defending.
  2. Use short passes to keep possession rather than long hopeful balls.
  3. Time your tackles — mistimed ones leave you out of position.
  4. Communicate with teammates about who presses and who covers.
  5. Practice your shooting angles; the physics reward precision.
