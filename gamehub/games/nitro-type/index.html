About Nitro Type

Nitro Type is a typing game that turns practice into a race. Type accurately to speed up your car and beat opponents to the finish line. With cars to unlock, achievements, and competitive races, it is a fun and motivating way to improve your typing.

How to play

Join a race and type the displayed text as accurately and quickly as possible. Correct typing accelerates your car; mistakes slow it down. Race against other players in real-time. Earn rewards and unlock new cars as you win. The goal is to be the fastest typist on the track.

Game modes

Real-time multiplayer typing races with progression and rewards.

Tips and tricks

  1. Focus on accuracy first — mistakes cost more time than they save.
  2. Practice regularly to build muscle memory.
  3. Use the results screen to identify problem words.
  4. Racing against faster players helps you improve.

Features

  • Typing races with cars and rewards.
