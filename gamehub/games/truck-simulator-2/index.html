About Truck Simulator 2

Truck Simulator 2 expands the haulage experience with more routes, heavier loads, and a deeper sense of what it takes to run a trucking operation. Navigate busy roads, manage your speed and braking over long distances, and deliver every cargo safely to its destination. It is a calm, methodical driving game built for players who enjoy the rhythm of the road and the satisfaction of a perfect delivery.

How to play

Select a job and take control of your truck, then follow the route to your delivery point. Traffic, tight turns, and long braking distances demand steady hands. Damage your cargo or your rig and the payout suffers, so drive with care rather than aggression. Successful deliveries earn money that unlocks better trucks and more challenging contracts, letting you build a proper trucking career one route at a time.

Game modes

Single-player delivery gameplay with progressive contracts and free driving between jobs.

Tips and tricks

  1. Maintain a safe following distance — you cannot stop on a dime with a full load.
  2. Use the full width of the road on wide turns to avoid clipping the trailer.
  3. Reduce speed before entering junctions rather than braking mid-turn.
  4. Check your route before you set off so you are not making last-second turns.
  5. Prioritize a damage-free delivery over a fast one for better rewards.

Features

  • Browser-based truck driving simulation with cargo hauling.
  • Multiple routes with traffic and road hazards.
  • Progression system that rewards careful, damage-free driving.
  • HTML5 powered, so it loads instantly with no install.
  • Works on desktop and mobile devices.
