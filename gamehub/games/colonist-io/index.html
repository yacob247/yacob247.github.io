About Colonist.io

Colonist.io is a polished online version of the classic settlement and trading board game. Build settlements, trade resources, and race to victory points against friends or players worldwide. With its clean interface, fast matchmaking, and faithful rules, it is the best way to play this genre in your browser.

How to play

Set up a game with friends or match with other players. Roll dice to gather resources based on your settlements. Trade resources with other players or the bank. Build roads, settlements, and cities to expand and earn victory points. Use development cards strategically. The first player to reach the target victory points wins.

Game modes

Online multiplayer with 1v1, 3-player, and 4-player matches, plus custom rules.

Tips and tricks

  1. Diversify your resource production rather than relying on one number.
  2. Trade aggressively early to build faster.
  3. Keep an eye on other players' victory points.
  4. Use development cards to swing the game at key moments.
