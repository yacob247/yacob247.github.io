About Taming.io

Taming.io blends survival, combat, and creature collection into one continuous multiplayer world. You start by gathering resources and building a base, then tame wild animals that fight alongside you, gather for you, and grow stronger as you do. With other players competing and cooperating around you, every session is a race to build, defend, and thrive.

How to play

Gather wood, stone, and food to survive, then build structures to protect yourself and your resources. Tame animals by meeting their requirements, and they will follow you, fight for you, and help you gather. Level up your character and your pets to unlock better gear and stronger abilities. Other players can attack your base or your pets, so you need defenses and allies. Expand, upgrade, and stake out your territory in a persistent world.

Game modes

Multiplayer survival and combat with taming, base building, and progression.

Tips and tricks

  1. Tame a combat pet early so you can fight and gather with backup.
  2. Build walls around your key resources before you start upgrading.
  3. Keep food in reserve — starving mid-fight is a quick way to lose everything.
  4. Level your pets by using them; stronger pets make every fight easier.
  5. Ally with other players when possible; solo survival gets rough later on.

Features

  • Survival gameplay with base building and resource gathering.
  • A taming system with many animals that fight and gather for you.
  • Multiplayer world with other players to fight or ally with.
  • Character and pet progression with upgrades.
  • Free to play in the browser.
