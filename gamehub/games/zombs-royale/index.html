About Zombs Royale

Zombs Royale takes the battle royale formula and makes it fast, colorful, and instantly playable in your browser. Drop in, loot up, and fight in a top-down world where matches move quickly and the action never stops. With a huge variety of weapons and many game modes, it is one of the most accessible and replayable royales on the web.

How to play

Choose a mode and drop onto the map. Search buildings and crates for weapons, ammo, shields, and healing items. The safe zone steadily shrinks, pushing all remaining players together. Fight with your squad or alone, using cover and terrain to gain an edge. Manage your shields and health, swap weapons to suit the range of each fight, and be the last one standing. Different modes change the rules and pace, so there is always a fresh way to play.

Game modes

Multiple battle royale modes including solo and squad play, with varied rulesets and match sizes.

Tips and tricks

  1. Top up shields whenever you find them — they act as a second health bar.
  2. Carry one close-range and one long-range weapon to cover all situations.
  3. Stay near the edge of the safe zone to avoid being surrounded.
  4. In squads, share loot and revive teammates instead of looting alone.
  5. Do not chase kills into the storm — the zone kills faster than you think.

Features

  • Fast-paced 2D battle royale with many game modes.
  • A large arsenal of weapons and consumables.
  • Solo and squad play with reviving.
  • Playable in the browser on desktop and mobile.
