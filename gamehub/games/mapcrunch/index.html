About MapCrunch

MapCrunch is a simple but addictive exploration tool that teleports you to a random street view location anywhere in the world. Look around, figure out where you are, and explore the planet from your browser. It is a relaxing way to travel without leaving your seat.

How to play

Click the button to be dropped in a random location. Look around using the street view controls. Try to guess where you are, or just enjoy the scenery. You can also choose specific countries or regions to explore. There is no goal — it is all about discovery.

Game modes

Single-player random street view exploration.

Tips and tricks

  1. Look for signs and language clues to identify locations.
  2. Use the sun and vegetation to guess the region.
  3. Try the 'guess the location' challenge for a game.
  4. Explore specific countries for a more focused experience.

Features

  • Random street view exploration.
  • Real-world locations around the globe.
  • Simple, relaxing experience.
  • Free to use in the browser.
