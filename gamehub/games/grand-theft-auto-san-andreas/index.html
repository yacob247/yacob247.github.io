About Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas is one of the most ambitious open-world games ever made, and now it runs right in your browser. Spanning three sprawling cities and the countryside between them, it follows a man returning home to find his family in trouble and his neighborhood under threat. With deep customization, a huge map, and a story full of unforgettable characters, it remains the gold standard for sandbox crime games.

How to play

Complete story missions to advance the narrative, or roam freely across the entire state. Drive cars, ride bikes, fly planes, and sail boats as you move between Los Santos, San Fierro, and Las Vegas-inspired Las Venturas. Fight with fists, melee weapons, and firearms, and manage your character's stats, appearance, and territory. Side activities include racing, gambling, property management, and gang warfare. How you spend your time is entirely up to you.

Game modes

Single-player open-world campaign with extensive free-roam, side missions, and minigames.

Tips and tricks

  1. Eat and exercise regularly — your character's fitness affects speed and stamina in fights.
  2. Complete side missions early to unlock better weapons and vehicles.
  3. Learn the map's highways for fast travel between cities.
  4. Keep a fast vehicle at your safehouse for escape missions.
  5. Save after every major milestone so a failed mission never costs you progress.

Features

  • Three massive cities and huge stretches of countryside to explore.
  • A long story campaign with side missions and minigames.
  • Character customization including fitness, appearance, and skills.
  • Driving, flying, boating, shooting, and gang warfare in one game.
  • Playable in the browser via the BottleShip player.

Frequently asked questions

What is Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas?

It is a legendary open-world crime game set across three cities and surrounding countryside, featuring a long story campaign and total freedom to roam.

Can I play San Andreas in a browser?

Yes, this version runs in your browser through the BottleShip player without a traditional installation.

Absolutely — you can ignore the story entirely and explore, drive, and cause chaos across the whole map.
