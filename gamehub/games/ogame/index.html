About OGame

OGame is a space strategy game about building fleets, colonizing planets, and battling for control of the galaxy. Manage resources across multiple worlds, research advanced technologies, and compete with players in a persistent universe. It is a deep, long-term strategy experience.

How to play

Start on a home planet and build mines to produce resources. Research technologies to unlock new ships and buildings. Build fleets to raid other players and defend your own planets. Colonize new planets to expand your empire. Join an alliance for protection and coordinated attacks.

Game modes

Persistent online space strategy with fleets, alliances, and galaxy-wide conflict.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep resources low to avoid raids.
  2. Join an alliance early.
  3. Balance economy and fleet.
  4. Research new technologies consistently.

Features

  • Space empire-building strategy.
