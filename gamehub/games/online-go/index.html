About Online Go

Online Go (OGS) is the leading browser platform for playing Go, the ancient strategy board game. With real-time and correspondence games, tournaments, rankings, and a friendly community, it is the best place to learn and compete in Go online.

How to play

Create an account and start a game against a player of similar rank. Place stones on the board to surround territory and capture opponent stones. The rules are simple but the strategy is deep. Play real-time or correspondence games, join tournaments, and study games to improve.

Game modes

Real-time and correspondence Go games, tournaments, and ranked play.

Tips and tricks

  1. Learn basic life-and-death shapes early.
  2. Play longer games to develop reading skills.
  3. Review your games with stronger players.
  4. Focus on territory and influence balance.
