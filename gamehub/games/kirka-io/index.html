About Kirka.io

Kirka.io is a blocky browser FPS that captures the energy of classic voxel shooters while adding its own fast, competitive twist. With multiple game modes, smooth movement, and satisfying gunplay, it is easy to pick up but hard to put down. Whether you are sniping across a map or rushing in with a shotgun, every match is a fresh chance to prove your aim.

How to play

Choose a mode and spawn into a blocky battlefield. Pick your loadout from available weapons, then fight to complete your team's objective — capture zones, eliminate the enemy team, or hold positions. Movement is fast and vertical, so use the map's geometry to flank and surprise. Reload behind cover, communicate with teammates, and adjust your weapon choice to the range of each fight. The team that coordinates better usually wins.

Game modes

Multiple team-based modes including deathmatch, objective play, and more, with loadout customization.

Tips and tricks

  1. Keep your crosshair at head height while moving around corners.
  2. Strafe while firing to make yourself a harder target.
  3. Learn the map's flanking routes rather than always running down the middle.
  4. Swap to a close-range weapon when entering tight indoor areas.
  5. Reload immediately after every kill — fights often come in waves.

Features
