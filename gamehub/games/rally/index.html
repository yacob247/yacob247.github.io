About Rally

Rally is the original bat-and-ball duel, a minimalist arcade classic that started it all. Bounce the ball past your opponent's paddle while defending your own. Simple, competitive, and timeless.

How to play

Control a paddle on your side of the screen and bounce the ball back and forth with your opponent. If the ball passes your paddle, your opponent scores. First to the target score wins. The ball speeds up over time, increasing the challenge.

Game modes

Single-player against AI and local multiplayer.

Tips and tricks

  1. Aim for the edges of your opponent's paddle.
  2. Change the ball's angle by hitting it off-center.
  3. Stay near the middle for better coverage.
  4. Watch the ball, not your paddle.

Features

  • The original Pong-style gameplay.
  • Simple, fast matches.
  • AI and local multiplayer.
  • Free to play in the browser.
