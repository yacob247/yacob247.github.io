About Starblast.io

Starblast.io is a space shooter that mixes fast combat with mining, upgrades, and team strategy. Start in a fragile ship, mine asteroids for resources, and evolve your vessel into a powerful warship. Whether you are defending your team's base or diving into a heated dogfight, every decision about upgrades matters.

How to play

Spawn in space and mine asteroids to earn crystals, then spend them to upgrade your ship along different paths. Each upgrade tier unlocks new weapons and abilities. Fight enemy ships, defend your territory, and cooperate with your team to control the map. Balance mining and fighting — neglect one and your team suffers. The match ends when one team destroys the other's base or reaches the score goal.

Game modes

Team-based space combat with mining, ship progression, and base defense.

Tips and tricks

  1. Mine early to fund upgrades before the first big fight.
  2. Pick upgrades that fit your role — a miner and a fighter should upgrade differently.
  3. Stick near teammates; lone ships get picked off quickly.
  4. Defend your base when it comes under attack; losing it can end the match.
  5. Learn the upgrade tree so you do not waste crystals on the wrong path.

Features

  • Team-based matches with base defense.
  • Multiplayer combat in the browser.
  • Deep progression within each match.
