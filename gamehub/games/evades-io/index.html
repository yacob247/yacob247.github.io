About Evades.io

Evades.io is a co-op arena game where teamwork and hero abilities are the keys to survival. Pick a hero, join a team, and fight through waves of enemies and bosses in a game that rewards coordination and smart ability usage. With multiple heroes and challenging content, it is a great browser co-op experience.

How to play

Choose a hero with unique abilities, then join a team and enter the arena. Work together to defeat enemies, avoid hazards, and progress through increasingly difficult stages. Each hero has a specific role — damage, support, tank — so play to your strengths. Coordinate ability usage with teammates to survive tough fights. The further you go, the harder the challenges become.

Game modes

Co-op arena gameplay with hero selection and wave-based progression.

Tips and tricks

  1. Stick with your team — solo play in Evades.io is a quick death.
  2. Learn your hero's abilities and use them at the right moments.
  3. Communicate with teammates about when to push and when to retreat.
  4. Focus fire on dangerous enemies first.
  5. Do not be afraid to restart and try a different hero composition.
