About Slow Roads

Slow Roads is a relaxing endless driving simulator that lets you cruise through procedurally generated landscapes with no goals, no timers, and no pressure. Just you, the open road, and beautiful scenery. It is the perfect game to unwind with.

How to play

Get in the car and drive. There are no objectives — simply steer, accelerate, and enjoy the scenery. Adjust the time of day, weather, and landscape settings to change the mood. The world is generated endlessly, so you can drive forever. It is about the journey, not the destination.

Game modes

Single-player endless driving with customizable environment settings.

Tips and tricks

  1. Use the settings to change time of day and weather for variety.
  2. Try different landscapes for new scenery.
  3. Drive slowly and enjoy the view.
  4. Use headphones for a more immersive experience.

Features

  • Relaxing endless driving simulator.
