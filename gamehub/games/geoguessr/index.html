About GeoGuessr

GeoGuessr drops you somewhere in the world using street view imagery and challenges you to figure out where you are. With limited free plays, it is a thrilling geography game that tests your observation and deduction skills.

How to play

You are placed at a random street view location. Look around for clues — signs, architecture, vegetation, license plates, and more. Then place a pin on the world map where you think you are. The closer your guess, the more points you score. Play multiple rounds and compete for the highest score.

Game modes

Single-player and multiplayer location-guessing games with limited free plays.

Tips and tricks

  1. Look for language and writing on signs.
  2. Check the sun's position to determine hemisphere.
  3. Notice driving side and road markings.
  4. Use vegetation and terrain as clues.

Features

  • Street-view location guessing game.
  • Real-world imagery from around the globe.
  • Scoring based on accuracy.
  • Free limited plays available.
