About Powerline.io

Powerline.io is a snake-style multiplayer arena game with a neon twist. Grow your line by collecting power-ups and orbs, and cut off opponents to eliminate them. Fast, colorful, and competitive, it takes the classic snake formula and turns it into a multiplayer battle for dominance.

How to play

Control your line as it moves around the arena. Collect orbs and power-ups to grow longer and gain abilities. Cut off other players by circling them, forcing them to crash into your line. Avoid crashing into walls or other players yourself. The longer you get, the higher you climb on the leaderboard — but the easier you are to trap. Stay mobile and look for opportunities to cut off opponents.

Game modes

Multiplayer snake-style arena combat with a leaderboard.

Tips and tricks

  1. Do not get too greedy — a long line is harder to control.
  2. Circle smaller opponents to trap them against your body.
  3. Stay away from the arena edges when possible.
  4. Use power-ups strategically rather than grabbing them randomly.
  5. Watch for players trying to cut you off and turn away early.
