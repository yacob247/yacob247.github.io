Sands of Anubis

▶ Play now

Sands of Anubis is an Egyptian tile matching puzzle. Tap a tile to move it into your tray. Collect three matching tiles to clear them. Only tiles that are not covered by another tile can be picked, so plan your moves. If the tray fills up with 7 tiles, the level starts over. Play almost 5000 levels among the pyramids.
