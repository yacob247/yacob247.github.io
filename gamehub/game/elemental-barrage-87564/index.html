Elemental Barrage

Elemental Barrage is a casual volley shooter. Monsters march down the board row by row — drag back, aim and release a barrage of elemental balls that bounce through them. Grab +1 pickups: when your ball tray overflows, twin balls merge into mightier elementals with burn, freeze, chain lightning and more.
