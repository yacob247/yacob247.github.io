Rooster Ranch

▶ Play now

Rooster Ranch is a relaxing egg-matching puzzle on a sunny farm. Tap an egg to send it along the open path into your nest tray. Collect three eggs of the same color to hatch a cute chick. An egg can only leave the board when its path to the tray is clear, so plan every move. If the tray fills up, the level is lost.
