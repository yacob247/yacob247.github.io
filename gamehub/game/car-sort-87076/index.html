Car Sort

▶ Play now

Car Sort is a colorful parking puzzle game. Tap a garage to send its front cars onto the road, then match cars with garages of the same color. Plan your moves, keep an eye on road capacity, and clear increasingly challenging levels. Use helpful boosters when you need more space or a fresh arrangement.
