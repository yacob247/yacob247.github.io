4 in a Blow

▶ Play now

4 in a Blow is Connect 4 with power-ups. Everyone already knows the rules, so you can start playing immediately. Every turn charges up your power meter — spend it to block a column, swap two tokens, remove a token, or drop twice in a row. The one rule that keeps it fair: a power can never win the game on its own.
