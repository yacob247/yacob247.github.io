Pixel Feast

▶ Play now

Pixel Feast is a relaxing pixel-art puzzle. Every level hides a picture built from colored pixels, and your job is to eat it clean. Tap a block on the bottom row of the tank. It cracks open, its little eaters drop into a free slot, then run across the picture and munch every pixel that matches their color.
