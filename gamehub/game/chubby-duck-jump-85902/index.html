Chubby Duck Jump is a one-button charge-and-jump arcade game starring a chubby yellow duckling on a quest across an endless sky. Hold to charge the jump meter, release to leap to the next floating plank - charge too little and the duck splashes into the meadow below, hold too long and it overshoots into thin air.
