SIX FOLD

▶ Play now

SIXFOLD: a 3D snake in six panels. Steer one ink line over a paper cube. Every mark you eat adds a link and the line moves faster; cross an edge and the cube rolls, turning the panel under you into a wall. Six faces, no floor, no finish — only your own line can stop you, so bite yourself and the run ends.
