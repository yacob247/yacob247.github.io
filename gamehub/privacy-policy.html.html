Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameHub ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit envizion.work/gamehub.html and any related pages. By using our site, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

GameHub is a free online game portal. We do not host any game files ourselves — all games are embedded from third-party providers such as GameDistribution, GameDavinci, and others. This policy covers the GameHub website itself, not the games or services provided by third parties.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask for personal information. We do not operate our own analytics or tracking servers. The small amount of data we handle stays on your device:

  • localStorage — your likes and dislikes are stored locally so the player remembers your preferences.
  • sessionStorage — the currently opened game is cached in your browser tab so the player can restore it after reloads.

Neither of these is sent to us. Clearing your browser data will remove them.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Games

When you play an embedded game, that game may set cookies or use similar technologies. Third-party providers (GameDistribution, GameDavinci, and any other game source) may collect data such as your IP address, device type, and interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not control these practices.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the provider shown on each game. GameDistribution's policy is available at gamedistribution.com/privacy-policy.

4. Advertising

Some games display advertising before or during gameplay. Advertising may be personalised based on your interests and location. You can typically opt out through your browser settings, your device settings, or the provider's ad preference centre.

5. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to a third-party game, please contact that provider directly.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you by third-party game providers. Contact the relevant provider to exercise those rights.

7. Data Security

We serve the site over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of third-party services.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of GameHub means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@envizion.work.
