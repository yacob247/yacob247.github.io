How to promote this honestly

1. Use one useful comparison page

Link to this guide from relevant reviews, creator resources, and tool documentation. Each link should help a reader decide whether monetization is relevant to them.

2. Add your video when it is ready

A walkthrough can show the calculator, the account dashboard evidence, and the difference between a static banner and an intrusive format. Do not describe a payment as received until the payment is actually visible in your records.

3. Keep referrals disclosed

If a link may earn a referral commission, label it as a referral or sponsored link. Do not write an anonymous “comment” that is actually authored by the site owner, and do not publish testimonials without permission and a verifiable source.

4. Avoid mass-produced review pages

More URLs do not automatically mean more trust. A smaller set of genuinely useful pages, each with original evidence and a clear purpose, is a better long-term publishing strategy than dozens of near-duplicates.
