SpeedForge is a browser extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge. It speeds up video advertisements and provides a turbo downloader. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

1 What SpeedForge collects

SpeedForge does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. Everything the extension does runs locally in your browser. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server-side database of users.

2 Local data storage

The following data is stored locally in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leaves your device:

  • Your speed and download settings (playback rate, mute, auto-skip, connection count)
  • The floating panel's position, active tab, and hidden/expanded state
  • Whether the speed feature is currently enabled

This data is used only to remember your preferences between sessions. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension or clearing extension data in your browser settings.

3 Data sent to third parties

When you activate a download or a bookmarklet, SpeedForge shows a short advertisement provided by HilltopAds. Loading this ad causes your browser to contact HilltopAds' ad servers, which is standard for ad-funded tools.

No personal data about your browsing is sent by SpeedForge itself — only the ad request from HilltopAds' own code, which runs inside a sandboxed iframe with no access to your extension data, browsing history, or any other site.

HilltopAds' privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at hilltopads.com/privacy-policy.

4 Downloads

When you use the turbo downloader, your browser fetches the file you requested directly from the URL you pasted. SpeedForge never sees, stores, or relays the file. The file goes straight from the source server to your device.

5 Permissions

SpeedForge requests the following permissions, each used only for the feature described:

  • downloads — to save files to your device
  • offscreen — to assemble downloads in the background without interrupting your browsing
  • storage — to remember your settings locally
  • tabs — to show the floating panel on the current tab
  • <all_urls> — so the panel and downloader work on any page you choose to use them on

6 No analytics, no tracking

SpeedForge contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no third-party trackers other than the ad network used for the ad gate.

In short: SpeedForge does not know who you are, what you search for, or what you download. It only knows your settings — and those stay on your machine.

7 Children

SpeedForge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

8 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top will be updated. Continued use of SpeedForge after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
