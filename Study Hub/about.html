About the English Text Types Study Hub

Why we built these resources and how they can help you reach a Band 6 in HSC English.

Our Purpose

The English Text Types Study Hub brings together original, syllabus-aligned study packs for senior English students. Each pack covers a distinct text type — beginning with discursive writing and expanding to persuasive writing — with a complete guide and a ten-lesson program.

What We Offer

  • Complete guides that explain each text type, its features, structure and techniques, connected to NESA's Module C: The Craft of Writing.
  • Ten-lesson programs with stimuli, exam-style questions, starter sentences, worksheets, Band 6 sample answers and marking criteria.
  • Printable resources including worksheets, sample answers, checklists and practice exam papers.

Our Approach

Everything is written in a clear, student-friendly voice while staying true to the demands of the NSW English syllabus. We emphasise sincerity, balance and control — the very qualities that NESA rewards at the top of the marking scale. Where specific course requirements apply, we encourage you to always confirm details against the official NESA syllabus and your school's assessment task notifications.

Note: This is an educational resource. Always refer to the current official NESA syllabus as the authoritative source for assessment requirements.
