How the Two Sets Fit Together

In Years K–10, NESA groups text types by their primary communicative purpose — imaginative, informative and persuasive — and requires texts across the specific mediums. In Years 11 & 12 (Stage 6), senior English courses instead require students to experience a diverse range of text categories — prose fiction, nonfiction, poetry, drama, film, media, and digital/multimodal texts. The K–10 purpose lens matures into the senior category lens, with both anchored in the NSW Education Standards Authority (NESA) syllabus.
