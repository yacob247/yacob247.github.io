Purpose Groups Text Types

In the K–10 syllabus, text types are organised by their primary communicative purpose — imaginative, informative or persuasive — while the specific mediums (spoken, print, visual, media, multimedia, digital) cut across them. Most real-world texts blend purposes; teaching focuses on a text’s dominant intent.
