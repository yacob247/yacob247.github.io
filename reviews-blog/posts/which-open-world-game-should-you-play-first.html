Which Open World Game Should You Play First?

A practical guide to choosing between Red Dead Redemption 2, Cyberpunk 2077, GTA V, Ghost of Tsushima, and The Witcher 3.

Editorial analysis

Open-world games compete for dozens or hundreds of hours. Choosing the right first one matters because each asks for a different kind of attention.

#5 Choose GTA V for Variety

Heists, driving, satire, chaos, and online content make GTA V the broadest pick. It is less emotionally focused than some rivals but extremely easy to recommend.

#4 Choose Ghost of Tsushima for Beauty and Clarity

The world is readable, stylish, and approachable. It is a great pick if you want open-world structure without overwhelming systems.

Night City is dense, loud, and visually spectacular. It is strongest when you want atmosphere and flexible combat builds.

#2 Choose The Witcher 3 for Writing

If quest writing matters more than pure traversal, The Witcher 3 remains one of the safest recommendations in games.

#1 Choose Red Dead Redemption 2 for Immersion

No game creates a more convincing open world, but it asks you to accept slower pacing and deliberate animation.
