Best Story Games for New Players

Narrative-first games that are approachable, memorable, and worth playing even if you do not usually follow gaming discourse.

Advertisement

Editorial analysis

Story games are often the easiest entry point for people who want to understand why games matter. The best ones combine performance, pacing, and interaction so the player feels responsible for the emotional weight of the story.

#5 Mass Effect Legendary Edition

A full sci-fi trilogy where choices, companions, and political alliances carry forward across games. It is ideal for players who like ensemble casts and long-form worldbuilding.

Read the full review →
#4 God of War Ragnarok

A cinematic action story about parenthood, fate, and restraint. It is polished, accessible, and emotionally direct without giving up spectacle.

Read the full review →
#3 The Last of Us Part I

Still one of gaming's strongest arguments for performance-led storytelling. It is intense, linear, and deliberate, making it easy to follow but difficult to forget.

Read the full review →
#2 The Witcher 3

A huge RPG where side quests often carry the emotional weight of main stories. It rewards curiosity and patience more than mechanical mastery.

Read the full review →
#1 Baldur's Gate 3

The rare story game where the player can break, bend, or redirect scenes in meaningful ways. It is complex, but the payoff is enormous.

Read the full review →
Bottom line: For a first story game, choose based on time: The Last of Us for a focused experience, Mass Effect for a trilogy, Witcher 3 or BG3 for a long-term obsession.
