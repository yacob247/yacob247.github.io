AI models & system requirements

Everything runs privately inside your browser on your own GPU. Nothing is sent to a server. Choose Auto and the app loads whatever is needed, or download a specific model yourself.

Models

Auto mode uses only Qwen 2 1.5B for all requests — chat, writing, email, tools, documents, code. Qwen 3.5 4B is optional and is never used unless you choose it yourself. Sizes and requirements are approximate and depend on your browser and driver.

Good to know

Auto mode always uses Qwen 2 1.5B — no auto-switching, no 0.5B model. The 4B model never loads unless you pick it from the Models menu. Models are stored once in your browser and loaded from there next time. You can free space any time from your browser's site data settings.
