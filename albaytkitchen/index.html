Questions

Delivery questions

How is the delivery fee calculated?

When you enter your postcode, we locate it on the map and measure the driving distance from 4 Dale Grove, Mount Druitt. The delivery band for that distance is then applied. Food prices are not affected.

Do you deliver to my area?

Yes — we deliver across New South Wales. Distances up to 100 km use the bands above; beyond 100 km we quote individually at $0.60/km with a minimum of $59.90.

What is the cut-off for next-day delivery?

Orders placed before 2 pm are delivered the following day across the metro area, subject to stock. Slow-cooked dishes and platters require additional notice.

Can I collect instead?

Yes. Collection is available from 4 Dale Grove, Mount Druitt between 9 am and 5 pm, Tuesday to Sunday, at no charge.

What if I'm not home?

Our drivers will call on arrival. If there is no answer, chilled orders may be left only where you have given authority to leave.

About us

A family kitchen in Mount Druitt

Al Bayt began in 2016 as a home kitchen cooking mansaf for family gatherings in Western Sydney. Ten years on, we operate a registered commercial kitchen at 4 Dale Grove, Mount Druitt, and a pastry section, supplying households, offices and function venues across New South Wales.

Our cooking

Jordanian first, Levantine throughout

Our kitchen is built around the Jordanian table — mansaf cooked in jameed, maqluba turned out of the pot, freekeh with chicken, musakhan on taboon bread, kibbeh in all its forms — and extends across the wider Levant: Syrian mezze, Lebanese sweets, Palestinian kunafa.

We do not use shortcuts. Jameed is imported from Karak. Tahini is Lebanese and stone-ground. Sumac and za'atar are sourced from a single grower. Bread is baked on site every morning, and nothing is fried in advance of an order.

Support

Frequently asked questions

How does pricing by the kilogram work?

Every dish on the menu has a price per kilogram. When you choose a weight — 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg and so on — the price shown is that proportion of the kilo rate. Platters and trays are sold as complete units.

What is the minimum order?

$60 across all delivery areas. There is no minimum for collection from the Mount Druitt kitchen.

How is delivery calculated?

Delivery is based on the road distance from 4 Dale Grove, Mount Druitt to your postcode. Enter your postcode at checkout and the fee updates automatically. Food prices never change.

How far in advance should I order?

Standard items: 24 hours. Platters: 48 hours. Events up to 100 guests: 72 hours. Larger functions: five working days to two weeks depending on size.

Is everything halal?

Yes. All meat and poultry is certified halal and sourced from an accredited butcher. Certificates are available on request.

How long will the food keep?

Cold mezze and salads: 3 days refrigerated. Cooked mains: 3 to 4 days refrigerated. Oriental sweets: 7 to 10 days in a sealed container. Breads are best on the day of delivery.

Can you cater for allergies?

We can advise on allergens and accommodate nut-free and gluten-free requests where practical, but the kitchen handles sesame, tree nuts, wheat, dairy and eggs. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Can I order for the same day?

Same-day delivery is not available. Collection from Mount Druitt may be possible for stock items if ordered before 11 am — please call the kitchen to confirm.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card online, bank transfer for orders above $300, and card or cash on delivery. Trade accounts are invoiced monthly on 14-day terms.

Can I return platters and trays?

Yes. A refundable deposit is charged on platters and hot trays, refunded when they are returned clean within seven days.

Do you do custom cakes?

Yes — minimum 1 kg, 72 hours' notice. Choose from our signature flavours or discuss a bespoke design with the pastry chef.

How do I become a wholesale customer?

Email the kitchen with your business name, ABN and expected weekly volume, and we will send a trade price list and sample arrangement.

Terms of service

1. Orders

All orders are subject to acceptance and availability. The minimum order value for delivery is $60. Orders are confirmed by email; an order is not accepted until confirmation is issued.

2. Pricing

All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Products are sold by net weight; weights are approximate within 3% and are recorded at packing. Delivery fees are calculated by road distance from 4 Dale Grove, Mount Druitt NSW 2770.

3. Delivery

Delivery windows are estimates. We are not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, or events outside our control. Risk passes to the customer on delivery or on placement in an agreed authority-to-leave location.

4. Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled up to 24 hours before the scheduled delivery time for a full refund. Platters and catering orders require 48 hours' notice. Custom cakes are non-refundable once production has begun.

5. Food safety

Food must be refrigerated immediately on receipt and consumed within the shelf life stated on the label. We accept no liability for food held outside temperature control after delivery.

6. Allergens

Our kitchen handles sesame, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, dairy and egg. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment and customers with severe allergies order at their own risk.

7. Returns

Any issue with an order must be reported within 24 hours of delivery. Platter and tray deposits are refunded on return of clean equipment within seven days.
